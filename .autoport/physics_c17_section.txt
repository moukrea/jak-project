WHY THIS CYCLE EXISTS
---------------------
The owner rejected the 10:12 build in full — "les pans de veste NE BOUGENT PAS DU TOUT, zero
physique ; le col est completement statique ; les meches de Keira sont ANCREES ; le noeud du
maire aucune physique ; la poitrine de Keira completement statique" — while this report's own
floors read hairrun=619.7 and chestrun=352.5. Both were true at once, and the reason is a
definition, not a tuning:

  `crun` measured `dev` = |pos - REST POSE|. A chain welded at a CONSTANT offset of 352 units
  scores 352 while visually soldered in place. That is why chestrun printed 352.4841, byte for
  byte, on every reading for three days: a deterministic constant, not a measurement.

Every calm ceiling in this file is one-sided — a dead chain maximises all of them — so the three
motion suppressors could kill the simulation to completion without reddening a single gate. The
whole week of greens rests on that one wrong definition.

Two things follow, and this cycle is both of them.

1. THE SOLVER AND ITS INSTRUMENTS WERE REWRITTEN (SPEC 17)
----------------------------------------------------------
`phys-slot-step!` went from 4806 lines to @@STEPLINES@@, restructured into the four steps the spec
asked for — integrate, constrain, collide, write the bone — and every motion suppressor was
REMOVED rather than retuned. Verified absent (defined constants with no reader remain only as
tombstones): sleep (PHYS-SLEEP-VEL), ring-down (PHYS-RINGDOWN-*), arrival/hysteresis
(PHYS-ARRIVE-*), the jerk cap (PHYS-JERK-CAP), sustained-contact velocity discharge
(PHYS-SPEND-SOFT, PHYS-STICK), the model-pose ladder (PHYS-MODELPULL) and `colskip`. The calm
rest state survives ONLY as a measurement — it holds nothing.

ONE suppressor was kept, deliberately and named: authored-animation priority. It is the owner's
own rule 5 ("les animations originales ont la priorite"), and Keira's goggles held to her eyes at
the Zoomer are its non-regression case. It is now quantified rather than assumed — see below.

THE ONE PRIMARY QUANTITY IS THE WRITTEN JOINT POSITION, FRAME BY FRAME.
Everything else derives from it, and nothing in the new instrument descends from anything else:
  motion  = |pos(t) - pos(t-1)| of the joint this pass WROTE, differenced against the AUTHORED
            pose so the character's own travel divides out (carrying hair along while Jak runs is
            not motion of the hair)
  inertness = the spread of that offset over frames the chain's own anchor was being driven.
            Near zero while the actor moves = welded, and it fails the leg BY NAME.
  jerk    = worst |delta pos| in one frame
  penetration = a written link inside the character's real skinned surface

THE MOTION FLOORS ARE REDEFINED, and their old magnitude form is retired from the ratchet so it
can never pass again. `hairrun` and `chestrun` are now the PATH the written joint travelled over
a window's locomotion frames — sum of the per-frame variation, worst window. A constant offset
contributes exactly 0.

THE THREE SUPPRESSORS, AS A PERCENTAGE OF FRAMES
------------------------------------------------
The spec's bar: anything above ~20 % of chain-frames is the culprit and gets loosened. The
denominator (`simcf`) had a defect of its own this cycle and it is worth recording, because it is
the same family as everything else here: simcf was incremented at TWO sites over the same set of
chain-frames, so it read DOUBLE and every share printed at HALF its true value — a suppressor
holding 30 % of frames would have shown 15 % and passed. And `clampcf` had NO WRITER AT ALL: it
printed a hard zero meaning "the collision clamp never acts", while the bound that does act
(`maxcorr`) was incrementing a different counter with no denominator. Both are fixed here; the
shares below are real fractions of real chain-frames.

2. THE "MESH-DERIVED" VOLUMES WERE THROWN AWAY TOO (SPEC 18)
-------------------------------------------------------------
The owner questioned the collision volumes and he was right. Measured over the 1335 fitted
volumes: median radius 967 units, p90 2756, max 13646 — a 3.3 m radius on a 2.3 m character,
with twelve volumes larger than the character itself.

It was invisible because the quality metric was a TAUTOLOGY. `fit-error` asked whether a volume
fitted to a bone's own vertices contained those vertices. It does, by construction. It read 0.000
for a week and never once asked the only question that matters — whether the volume follows the
SURFACE where other chains actually pass. So "mesh-derived" never meant more than "radius
computed automatically": the same capsule representation the owner has rejected from day one, and
a capsule cannot be a shoulder, a jaw, or a flared trouser leg.

THE MEASURE IS RETIRED. Any collider-quality number that compares a volume to itself is banned
from this report. The historical `fit-error = 0.000` lines in the cycle-6 and cycle-13 sections
below are superseded and should be read as what they were: true, and meaningless.

WHAT REPLACES IT: COLLISION AGAINST THE REAL SKINNED SURFACE.
Per body bone, real skinned-mesh sample points with their outward normals are taken offline from
the same merc geometry that is drawn, stored as an offset from the bone origin in that bone's own
axes — the identical convention the existing link samples already use — and at runtime each set
rides its bone's live matrix, so in any pose the samples ARE the body's surface.
The test is a chain point against the real surface of the skinned mesh — a SIGNED DISTANCE taken
along the nearest sample's outward normal, negative meaning inside the character. That number
cannot be true by construction, which is the entire reason for it.

TWO JUDGEMENTS MADE HERE, STATED RATHER THAN BURIED:
  * The capsules SURVIVE, demoted to BROAD PHASE — they say which volumes are near, the surface
    says whether a point is inside the body. That is the role they can actually fill, and it is
    why their radii now carry a sanity bound instead of a containment guarantee. The fitter's
    exact-containment LP was replaced by a robust one (trim the far tail, then a hard cap): the
    9 models that carried oversized volumes were refitted, and no volume anywhere now exceeds
    @@MAXRADIUS@@ units. The other 51 models are byte-identical.
  * The LINK RADIUS is deliberately NOT added to the surface test. A nearest-sample signed
    distance has an error of the order of the sample spacing; asking it to hold a strand a full
    link radius clear of a coarsely sampled torso would push chains OFF the body, which is the
    "it floats" failure the owner rejects as firmly as the clipping. The surface answers the
    question it can answer exactly — is this point inside the character — and the link's own
    thickness stays with the broad phase, where it already was. Density is the precision level's
    lever, and unlike a capsule this is an approximation whose error SHRINKS when the level buys
    more samples.
