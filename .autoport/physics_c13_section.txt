CYCLE 13b — THE THREE LICENCES WERE GONE AND THE PERIMETER STILL HAD HOLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Before this build was offered to the owner, the cycle-13 solver was read back adversarially
against its own claim. It did not survive it. Three more ways for a strand to pass through
its own character UNCOUNTED were found in the code that was supposed to have closed them,
and all three are fixed here. They are listed first because the cycle-13 text below would
otherwise be making a promise the code did not keep.

  1. `self?` EXCLUDED BODY VOLUMES, NOT JUST THE STRAND'S OWN (jak-hd-physics.gc:1861).
     The one exclusion cycle 13 kept was "a volume fitted to this chain's own joints is this
     chain's own geometry". It was implemented as "EITHER end sits on ANY joint of the chain"
     — and the offline fitter emits BODY spans that TERMINATE on a chain root: `neckB Learbase`
     r=1175, `hips shirtLthigh` r=1912, `chest RchestDanglerA` r=13646. Under the either-end
     rule those are dropped for the ear, the jacket pendant and the chest dangler respectively.
     Counted over the whole file, twice and independently: 532 (chain, volume) pairs excluded by
     the either-end rule against 254 by the both-ends rule, so 278 real body volumes come back,
     across 230 of the 345 chains. A volume that never enters `cclist` is invisible to the solver,
     to the model-pose fallback AND to the residual audit alike, so `resid` would have read 0 with
     a strand inside the neck. It sat on exactly the geometry the owner names — ears, jacket
     flaps, danglers.
     Scope stated honestly: no chain lost its ENTIRE perimeter to the old rule (the worst case
     kept 6 of 7 volumes; jak-hd's worst chain kept 78 of 81). So this is a hole in coverage, not
     a chain with nothing to hit, and it cannot be the whole of what the owner sees.
     FIXED: own now means BOTH ends are the chain's (or, for a one-bone collider, its single
     joint). One end on the body makes the volume the BODY's, however it is named. The root link
     that shares the joint is not shoved out of it — the model-pose floor is per (link, volume)
     and reads ~0 there — while the TIP, whose model pose is nowhere near, is caught in full.

  2. AN ANIMATOR HOLDING A CHAIN TURNED OFF ITS COLLISION *AND* ITS AUDIT (:2284, deleted).
     `(when (> authw 0.5) (set! collide? 0))` existed for a real reason — Keira pulling her
     goggles over her eyes puts them inside the head capsule on purpose — but it removed the
     whole chain-frame from the residual audit, and it fires on every chain with an authored
     channel: jak-hd `earL`/`earR` (authored=0.35), keira-hd `goggles` (authored=1.0). Those are
     three of the six sites the owner listed by name.
     FIXED: collision stays on always. The authored pose is protected by the model-pose floor
     instead — the per (link, volume) cap now takes the nearer of the MODELLED and the AUTHORED
     pose — so the animator is still never fought, and every direction he is not holding stays
     guarded and stays counted.

  3. THE STRAND-VS-STRAND PASS RAN AFTER THE AUDIT AND WROTE THE BONES (:4650-4790).
     It executes after the body resolve, after the model-pose fallback and after the residual
     audit, and it writes `out`, `pos`, `prev` and the bone transform directly. So a link the
     fallback had just parked clear of every volume could be shoved straight back into the
     character, with nothing testing or counting it: the "cannot fail" guarantee was void by
     ordering, not by arithmetic.
     FIXED: a strand push that would DEEPEN a link's own body penetration is refused for that
     link (`xveto`) and the separation is taken entirely by the partner; if neither may move the
     contact is left overlapping and COUNTED (`xunres`, `xunresmax`) instead of being resolved
     into the character's mesh. The comparison is before-vs-after on the same link and the same
     volume, so the model-pose cap cancels and a strap RESTING against a capsule is never vetoed.
     Also: strand-vs-strand had no residual counter at all — only contacts it had handled — which
     is the same blind spot as a body audit with no perimeter. It has one now.

  4. THE DEFAULT PRECISION LEVEL DROPPED A WHOLE CLASS OF CHAIN (physics_chains.txt [levels]).
     `level 1` — the default, named FULL — carried `classmask=3`, primary+secondary only, so all
     43 `class=accessory` chains were declared and permanently inert at the setting the owner
     plays on: 4 `earL` + 4 `earR`, Samos' `logL`/`logR` (the bun he asked for by name), horns,
     fur, belts, pouches, the earring, the shackle. "Declared and not running" is the exact
     failure he called out in cycle 3b. FIXED to `classmask=7`; LIGHT (level 0) keeps
     `classmask=1`, so the cost escape route stays one notch down where he put it.

  5. `nomask` WAS COUNTING THE WHOLE ACTOR ON EVERY FRESH BINDING (jak-hd-physics.gc:1389).
     The device run before these fixes failed on `nomask=17` — "a live chain reached no volume at
     all" — identically in two different scenes, and my first explanation for it was the `self?`
     bug above. That explanation was WRONG, and it is recorded here rather than quietly dropped
     because a plausible wrong cause is how a cycle gets spent fixing the wrong thing.
     The real cause: cycle 13 changed what `cncol` MEANS — from "how many volumes name this chain"
     (a property of the data, true every frame) to "how many this chain reached" (defined only on
     frames it was simulatable) — and moved the per-window reset to a -1 "never asked" sentinel,
     but left the BIND path setting it to 0. So on the first window of every binding each chain
     read `cncol == 0` and was counted. The evidence is in the same log line: jak-hd `nomask=17` of
     17 chains, dax-hd `nomask=5` of 5, alongside `nopose=5100` (17 chains x 300 frames un-posed)
     and `ccnsum=0` — a window in which the perimeter rebuild had not run once, so there was
     nothing to have reached. Every other window in both legs read `nomask=0`.
     FIXED to -1. This is a false positive removed, not a metric narrowed to pass: an un-posed
     parked-spawn frame is one where the chain is not simulated, so it can neither reach a volume
     nor clip through one, and `nopose` still reports those frames in its own field.

  6. AND ONE THAT IS NOT FIXED, STATED RATHER THAN BURIED: the per-chain reach sphere is measured
     from THIS frame's poses plus PHYS-REACH-MARGIN (512 units). A chain whose own collision
     correction can exceed that in one frame — possible for chains longer than 1024 units — could
     reach a volume it was not tested against on that frame. `cctrunc` cannot see it (it counts
     list overflow, and the largest model declares 83 volumes against a list of 96, so it can
     never fire on this data — a green `cctrunc` proves nothing here and is not offered as if it
     did). This is narrow and it is unmeasured; it is written down so the next cycle starts from
     it instead of rediscovering it.

RESULT: (cycle 13) the owner's verdict on the cycle-12 build was "c'est TOUJOURS BROKEN",
and he was right for a reason no amount of tuning could have reached. The residual audit had
no PERIMETER to be residual over. Three separate licences let a strand pass through a body
without ever being counted, and all three are gone in this build:

  1. `colskip=` — 202 of the 345 chains carried it, including 37 `earL` and 36 `earR`, the
     exact geometry he watches strands go through. A link that skips collision cannot register
     a penetration, so those chains left the audit by construction. `resid = 0` was
     arithmetically exact and completely false. RETIRED: the key is no longer an exemption,
     and `cs` is now exactly `rootlock` — the only links exempt are the ones that do not move
     because they ARE the bone. What colskip was hand-authoring (a link whose MODEL pose is
     inside a volume must not be shoved out of it) is computed at runtime instead, per
     (link, volume), every frame, by the model-pose floor, which also tracks the animation
     instead of being frozen into a data key.

  2. `chains=` — all 2384 volumes carried one, and it was worse than a filter. The offline
     fitter was emitting a SEPARATE, differently-shrunk copy of each body span PER CHAIN:
     1004 real spans had become 2374 generated lines, 2.36 variants each, radii spread up to
     20x on the same span (`assistant-lod0`, span `head flipR1`: 40 / 81 / 564 / 644 / 805).
     Every chain therefore had its own private body, and where a chain's private copy had been
     shrunk under the emit floor it had no volume there AT ALL. Counted: 353 of the volumes had
     a hole in them for at least one chain, and 85 existed for nobody — they were never written
     to the file. RETIRED: the generator now emits ONE volume per span at its fitted mesh
     radius (1335 lines, down from 2384), and which chain tests which volume is decided at
     runtime by REACH — a bounding sphere measured from this frame's poses against every volume
     of the character. A volume is excluded only when it is out of reach, and the reach is
     measured, never declared. That is the owner's rule read literally: "le filtre ne peut
     qu'exclure ce qui est geometriquement INATTEIGNABLE, et cette inatteignabilite doit etre
     PROUVEE".

  3. `xchain=` — strand-vs-strand ran only between pairs the data had introduced to each other.
     He called this the worse half ("et encore plus les elements a physique avec d'autres
     elements a physique") and named four pairs. RETIRED: every pair on the actor is tested
     unless the two bounding spheres measured this frame cannot touch.

AND THE FIT-ERROR THAT WAS QUOTED AT HIM WAS MEASURED ON THE WRONG OBJECT. The offline audit
grades the FITTED radii; the file shipped the per-chain SHRUNKEN copies. So "fit error 0.000"
was true of volumes that were never written to disk. This is the first run in which the audited
object and the emitted object are the same object: max fit-error = 0.000 and max whole-mesh hole
= 0.000 across all 43 model sections, on the 1335 volumes that actually ship, with the offline
positive control firing on 43 of 43 models (injected depths 615-10533 units).

THE ONE EXCLUSION THAT SURVIVED, AND WHY IT IS NOT A LICENCE. `chains=` was also carrying,
implicitly, "a chain does not collide with its own bones" — the fitter emits a volume per bone
span, so a chain's own links each get one fitted to their own vertices, and 527 of the 1335
volumes have a chain joint at one end. That exclusion is kept and made STRUCTURAL rather than
authored: a volume is skipped for a chain only when one of its two ends IS one of that chain's
own joints. It cannot be widened by data and it cannot hide another chain's volume or a body
volume. The model-pose floor alone would not have covered it — it zeroes the volume for the
link that OWNS it, and says nothing about the strand's other links, which would then have been
shoved out of their own strand.

THE POSITIVE CONTROL NOW CONTROLS THE PERIMETER, NOT THE COUNTER. This is the precise
distinction the cycle-12 rejection turned on, and it was a fair hit: the old control dragged
each link toward `cclist[0]`, the first volume the chain was ALREADY tested against, so it
could only ever prove that the counter is able to count. A correct counter over a perimeter
that excludes half the chains yields a zero that is exact and false — the fourth vacuous zero
of this phase, after resid/push, idledrift/idlewin and restdevA/restwin. The injection now aims
at the volume of the WHOLE ACTOR nearest to the link, chosen with no reference to the chain's
perimeter. If the perimeter is complete the link is driven into a volume that is being tested
and push=/resid= climb with injected=; if there is a hole, injected= climbs ALONE, which names
the hole instead of hiding it. Measured, deliberate injection, ARMED vs DISARMED:

`hang=` ON FAMILY A DOES NOT MOVE THE REST POSE, AND THAT IS CODE, NOT PROSE. 157 of the
family A chains still carry `hang>0` and the owner's rule W says a body part's idle pose is the
model's, "pas plus haut, pas plus bas". Both are true because the gravity rest-pull is scaled by

HOW `resid` IS REACHED, SAID PLAINLY, BECAUSE IT CHANGES WHAT IT PROVES. The frame no longer
ends on an iteration count. After the iterative repair, every free link is walked from where the
solver left it toward its own MODEL pose and takes the SMALLEST step that clears every volume in
its perimeter — a point that is clear in closed form, because each volume's effective radius is
capped at the model pose's own distance to that volume's spine. `pos` and `prev` take the
identical delta, so it injects exactly zero velocity (owner rule O), and a link that is already
clear is not moved at all. It stands down under `inject=`, or the owner's positive control would
be silently repaired into a fifth empty zero.
  So `resid = 0` no longer says "the solver converged". It says "the fallback held". The numbers
  that carry the real information are `mfsnap` (how many link-frames needed the fallback at all),
  `mfsnapmax` (the largest single correction — a per-frame positional jump, so a large one is a
  visible twitch) and `mfhard` (the times not even the model pose was clear, which the closed-form
  guarantee does not cover). If `mfsnap` were a large fraction of the link-frames, this would be
  physics being overwritten by the model pose — the owner's cycle-8 complaint AJ — and it would
  need to be read as such rather than as a clean audit. Those three are in the per-leg lines below.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED
  * WHETHER IT LOOKS RIGHT. Every aesthetic judgement in this phase is the owner's, and no
    capture campaign was run — that is a permanent rule of this phase, not an omission. What
    changed here is structural: chains that were never tested are now tested. Whether the
    result reads well on screen is his call, and section 13 lists what to look at.
  * THE PER-FRAME COST WENT UP AND IS NOT DISGUISED. The inner loop used to walk an authored
    list of a handful of volumes; it now walks everything the chain can reach, up to the
    model's full count (83 on the largest, `eichar-lod0`). The precision level still arbitrates
    it — the tier gate is applied inside the perimeter rebuild, so a lower level tests fewer
    volumes, which is the owner's own escape route for cost. It is his call whether the top
    level is too expensive on the phone.
  * THE METAL RING on Jak's chest-plate and the BUCKLE on his back strap are still not
    delivered, by measurement rather than omission: no rig in the 458 shipped carries a joint
    for either, so both need a bone injected at HD prep plus a weight transfer. That is an
    asset-pipeline job, unchanged by this cycle.
  * THE STRAND-VS-STRAND RESIDUAL IS NEW AND IS NOT YET ZERO BY DESIGN. `xunres` counts contacts
    the frame could not clear, and refusing to resolve one INTO the body is a deliberate choice
    (the owner's blocker outranks strand separation). A nonzero `xunres` is therefore a real
    reading, not a failure to be tuned away — but it is also the number that says two strands are
    still overlapping, so it is his to look at.
