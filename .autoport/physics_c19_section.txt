THE DATA IS GENERATED, NOT PATCHED (SPEC 7)
--------------------------------------------
Keira's chains are no longer hand-written anywhere. `.autoport/physics_keira_gen.py` is the
contract made executable, and it is the file to read rather than the data:

  * THE CHAIN SET is discovered in her RIG. Joint order and hierarchy come from
    recharged_assets/hd_anim/<model>-k2e.json (`rows`, HD rig order, each row carrying
    hd_name + hd_parent — the same sidecar the retargeter uses); a KIND table names which
    joint STARTS a chain and the rest of the chain is read off the hierarchy by walking
    children, so a chain cannot disagree with the rig it runs on.
  * THE FAMILY IS DERIVED from the kind. What she IS — bangs, mid hair, back hair, ears,
    chest — is family A: physics live at all times, gravity on the DYNAMICS only, and hang=0
    by construction, so the spring's own equilibrium is the model pose and it returns there
    by being a spring. What she WEARS — goggles, knee flaps, trouser flaps, shoulder straps
    — is family B: it hangs, hang>0 by construction, and it is NEVER pulled back to the
    model pose. There is no way to write a hair chain into family B in that file.
  * THE ROOT IS LOCKED BY CONSTRUCTION: every chain of two joints or more is emitted
    rootlock=1, so its first link rides the carrier bone rigidly. `rootlock=0` on a strand is
    not expressible. That is SPEC 3 — "la racine suit RIGIDEMENT l'os porteur, elle ne derive
    pas" — and it reverses by construction what the previous cycle did by hand when it
    unlocked four bang roots to satisfy a motion gate and detached her hair from her skull.
  * THE PER-LINK RADIUS IS MEASURED off the skinned mesh — the inner quartile of the
    perpendicular spread of the vertices each link owns, derived from the merc geometry and
    never hand-tuned — and measured POST-RESKIN, on the weights the bake actually draws.
    That last word was a real defect: on the donor, LpantFlap/RpantFlap and rBoob/lBoob are
    dominant on ZERO vertices, so the three-vertex floor fired and her trouser flaps shipped
    at radii=24 — 0.6 cm against a real half-thickness of ~362. A link with no thickness
    cannot be seen by another chain and cannot be held off a surface.
  * NO DEROGATION KEY IS EMITTED: no colskip, no chains= volume filter, no xchain= mask, no
    rootfree=. Two of those were already dead code in the solver (checked, not assumed:
    nothing reads *phys-cxmask* or *phys-colmask*, so the strand pass already pairs every
    chain of an actor geometrically and every volume is already tested against every chain
    it can reach). Removing a key with no reader changes no behaviour; it removes the false
    impression that the data may grant exceptions.
  * WHAT IS LEFT IS STYLE, and it is SPEC 4's three levers kept apart: fermete = stiffness
    with a near-zero stretch (never the damping — "amortir = tuer"), debattement = maxangle,
    vivacite = couple + mass.

KEIRA, PER NAMED CHAIN — THE FOUR-PART VERDICT SPEC 10 ASKS FOR
----------------------------------------------------------------
The delivery condition is one execution showing, per named chain: root ANCHORED + tip
MOVING + ZERO surface penetration with a positive control that fired + no visible jump.
All four are read off the same run, and all four descend from the one primary quantity SPEC
8 allows — the position WRITTEN to the bone, frame by frame.
  ROOT ANCHORED   rootdev, the drift of a root-locked link read BACK out of the bone matrix
                  after everything else in the frame has run. Must be ~0.
  TIP MOVING      cvar / cinr, the per-frame variation of the WRITTEN joint on the free
                  links and the TIP — never on the anchored root, and never |pos - rest|,

  NO PENETRATION  surfpen, the residual depth of the written mesh inside her REAL skinned
                  surface, with surftested as the confession count and a positive control.
  NO JUMP         resjerk, the worst single-frame resolution correction.

COLLISION — AGAINST THE REAL SURFACE, WITH THE POSITIVE CONTROL THAT FIRED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
SPEC 5: the collision is against the real skinned surface (signed distance to the surface),
not against proxy volumes; SPEC 18's `bs` sets are that surface — real skinned-mesh vertices
with their outward normals, per body bone, taken offline from the same merc geometry that is
drawn, riding their bone's live matrix so that in any pose the samples ARE her surface. That
is a mesh-derived envelope, not a capsule set with gaps: the collision volume approximates
the character MESH because it is sampled from it. The retired measure it replaces,
`fit-error`, asked whether a volume fitted to a bone's own vertices contained those vertices
— true by construction, a tautology — so the only admissible collider-quality number is the
chain-to-REAL-SURFACE signed distance, and it is what is reported here. Read `fit-error`
below as that quantity, which is what it was replaced by.

THE SUPPRESSOR LEDGER (SPEC 9) — IN DISPLACEMENT, NEVER IN FRAMES
------------------------------------------------------------------
SPEC 9 starts every motion suppressor OFF and admits one back only if a MEASURED defect
requires it, with a report of how much motion it REMOVED — not how many frames it ran on,
which is the wrong dimension. Checked in the solver rather than assumed, the suppressors
SPEC 9 names are already absent: PHYS-SLEEP-VEL, PHYS-RINGDOWN-*, PHYS-RING/-DAMP,
PHYS-ARRIVE-*, PHYS-HOLD-N, PHYS-XHOLD, PHYS-STICK*, PHYS-JERK-CAP and PHYS-MPMOVE are all
constants with NO READER anywhere in the file. The calm/rest state is a MEASUREMENT and
holds nothing. Four mechanisms are live, each required by the spec by name:
  * the collision resolve (SPEC 5) — bounded per frame, and its correction is mirrored into
    `prev` so the projection injects zero velocity, which is the owner's rule O and is what
    gives a chain a rest pose it can reach instead of a ring it cannot leave. A damped,
    bounded projection, never a hard re-projection each frame.
  * authored-anim priority (SPEC 6) — opt-in, and on Keira it is declared on exactly ONE
    chain, her goggles.
  * the family-A model pull (SPEC 2) — armed only after the chain has ALREADY been quiet for
    PHYS-RESTCALM frames, so it can remove drift that has stopped and never motion that
    exists, bounded per frame and velocity-neutral.
  * the spawn / big-transition settle ramp — burst is its bar and the bar is 0.
`remprod` is the displacement the integrator produced, |out0 - targ| over written links and
frames; `remclamp` is what the resolve took away, |out0 - out|; `remauth` is how far
authored priority moved the spring target; `remcalm` is the model pull's own step. Nothing
here counts frames and nothing is derived from a chain index.

@@SUPPRESSORS@@

AUTHORED ANIMATION HAS PRIORITY, PER CHAIN, AND GIVES THE JOINT BACK (SPEC 6)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Detection is strictly PER CHAIN and is taken on that chain's own authored offset as a
fraction of its own length — never on the actor, never on a global or parent signal, so
unrelated bones moving cannot suspend a chain's physics. Above the threshold a blend weight
ramps IN at authrise per second, the target becomes the authored pose and the chain stops
colliding; when the authored motion stops the weight ramps OUT at authfall per second and
the physics RESUMES progressively from where it is, the target sliding back to the calm rest
pose, so there is no snap by construction — a blend back in, not a switch.
HELD STILL IS NOT NO LONGER HELD, and this is armed so that it cannot be confused: the
signal is the LEVEL of the authored offset, with hysteresis at half the threshold, so a hand
that holds the goggles motionless at her eyes keeps them, and the release is NOT armed on the
authored channel's SPEED — a static hold does not read as "no animation". The owner's named
non-regression case is the Sandover Zoomer loop with the goggles held to the eyes, and it is
the X-MAX leg below: Keira at the Zoomer in village1, her goggles the only chain of hers
declaring authored=. Her ears do NOT declare it any more — this cycle's first build gave
them authored=0.40 on the strength of SPEC 1, and SPEC 9 overrides that: no measured defect,
no suppressor. Daxter's ears keep theirs, which is authored-anim priority proven on the
actor ND actually hand-keys, and his authored share is the rule working rather than a defect.
Legitimate exception, stated because it is not a penetration: when the animation puts the
goggles in front of her eyes their mesh IS inside the head volume on purpose, and the
authored floor is exactly what stops the resolve from shoving them out of her face.

@@SITES@@

THE CALM CEILINGS, ON THE SAME EXECUTION AS THE MOTION FLOORS
--------------------------------------------------------------
SPEC 9's rule is that calm may never be bought by killing motion, which is only checkable if
both are read off the same run. They are, below. Each ceiling carries the count that proves
it was sampled — `idlewin` for the drift, `restwin` for the model-pose fidelity, `restcf`
for the rest-state frames — because a zero next to a zero count is the empty kind this phase
has shipped five times. An unsatisfiable constraint must SETTLE and never oscillate: an idle
stretch still ringing after PHYS-SETTLE-BAR frames is counted as `unsettled`, the oscillation
count `oscn` / `oscmax` is taken on the WRITTEN bone, and the decay is what `settletime`
measures — frames from "the driving motion stopped" to "the chain is at rest", which no
re-scoping can hide because it measures DECAY rather than the absence of contact.
GRAVITY IS WORLD-SPACE, and it is measured rather than restated from the constant: `gdir` is
the vector the integrator actually applied, obtained as the difference between the
acceleration with and without the gravity term, and it must read (0, -1, 0) however the actor
is turned; `gloc` is the same vector in the anchor bone's own basis and must rotate, which is
what proves the first one is genuinely world and not local. `gsamp` is the count: family A
gates its gravity to zero on an upright actor, so gsamp = 0 means NOT MEASURED and is read
as such, never as a defect. MASS reaches the integrator — omega is divided by sqrt(mass), so
a heavier chain integrates at a lower frequency with a late start and a follow-through; it is
not a dead data key. The gravity rest-pull is REMOVED from BODY chains: hang is 0 on every
family A chain of hers by construction (hair, chest, ears), so gravity never moves their
equilibrium, only their dynamics. The owner's exception is implemented and is not silently
always on: `tiltmax` is how far from upright the actor got, and gravity's authority over a
family A rest pose is that number — hang and the gravity term are both scaled by it, so when
she is no longer upright gravity resumes.

@@SKINMAP@@

THE OWNER-NAMED CHAINS, ANSWERED BY NAME
-----------------------------------------
`cvar` is the mean per-frame variation of the WRITTEN joint on locomotion frames — a chain
welded at a constant offset scores 0 here no matter how far from its rest pose it is welded,
which is the entire point, and it is the temporal variation of the written bone rather than
|pos - rest|. `cinr` is the same quantity over the frames the chain's OWN anchor was driven
and is what the verdict is taken on (INERT below 0.25 over at least 30 samples). `cvn` /
`cish` are the sample counts: a zero next to them is UNJUDGED, never clean. `ctmin` is the
worst blend fraction the resolve held over the window and `ctz` the frames it would have
vetoed outright — the diagnosis kept live after its fix.

ONE THING ABOUT HOW THE NUMBERS ARE TAKEN, because it decides them: the device drives Jak
with `setprop debug.opengoal.cpad_inject`, and that TU is Android-only. The x86 leg
synthesizes a pad_replay v2 file instead (4 x 12 s of held stick, two jumps, an eight-way
turn). It matters more than it sounds: EVERY jak-hd chain reads INERT while he stands still
and MOVING once he is driven, so an undriven leg would have reported the owner's defect on
chains that do not have it — and missed it on Keira, who is an NPC and is never driven at
all. Her excitation is her own idle and her Zoomer work loop.
