== ELEMENT 2: hud-recharged ECO GAUGE (replaces stock hud-power) ==
   - New process hud-recharged-power; tally on remaining eco time exactly like stock hud-power;
     suppresses stock hud-power when ON; stock position (435,370), x/y-sgn +1 slide.
   - The gauge is a RING: draw jak_gauge_empty base quad; then the ACTIVE eco's full sprite
     (blue/red/yellow; green falls back to blue — no green asset) MASKED to the current fill by
     rhud-fan: a triangle-fan clip covering only the filled angular sector
     [6 o'clock .. 6 o'clock + 270deg * frac] where every vertex uv is the affine map of its
     position on the canvas (mask technique; the decorated bronze quadrant is the non-fill cap).
   - The _end TIP is authored at the full-gauge (3 o'clock) position on the same canvas: it is
     drawn as the full-canvas quad ROTATED around its own center by 270deg*(frac-1), which walks
     the tip along the ring to sit exactly at the fill edge (rotated tip technique).
   - frac = stock formula (eco-timeout - (frame - eco-pickup-time)) / eco-full-timeout, clamped;
     low-eco (<0.2) alpha blink at the stock logand-4 cadence for fill+tip.
   - Evidence: gauge_blue_full.png (full ring, drain recedes from the tip end — correct
     direction), gauge_blue_mid.png (half fill, clean angular mask cut + tip at the edge),
     gauge_red_mid.png, gauge_yellow_high.png (per-eco colors), gauge_low_a.png +
     gauge_low_blink.png (low-eco blink phases).
