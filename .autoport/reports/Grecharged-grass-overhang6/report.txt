## 1. What the owner rejected and what round 6 builds (his words -> geometry)
The v5 rim-drape ("complètement pourri, pas exploitable") hung blades from bare lip edges over dirt faces. Round 6 follows his exact spec:
- ZONE 1 "sur toute cette bordure (sans dépasser) l'herbe commence à s'incliner vers le vide": every walkable-top blade within 0.90m of a true-rim boundary edge is tagged (collapsed when ON) and replaced by a tail twin whose growth axis blends up->outward-horizontal by k*0.55 (k=1 at the lip). Direction comes from the nearest true-rim edge segment's outward dir (the v6 RimDrapeSeg table, repurposed); plane-capped at bake so no lean pokes an inner-corner wall. 65842 lean twins in training.
- ZONE 2 "un peu de mesh qui descend toujours avec l'herbe verte plate... des brins de plus en plus penchés dessus, en suivant EXACTEMENT cette partie": census truth — 1896 of 1897 sub-lip droop faces carry the native hang-alpha texture, so the flat-green descending mesh is the TRANSITION curl band (921 bit4 walkable tra-grass tris, 546 m2) whose blade BASES the LOCKED FLOORGAP/FLOORBELOW stack rightly culls (that is WHY the upright lawn stops at the boundary). Round 6 scatters comb-class (nspare=5+w) blades ON those tris in the toggle-gated tail, w = the smooth-normal tilt ramp — near-upright where the curl meets the lawn, fully bent where the mesh steepens: the mesh itself drives the "de plus en plus penchés" gradient. 32354 zone-2 blades.
- ZONE 3 "au moins deux couches d'herbe pour faire du volume, animée, qui tombe complètement vers le bas, recouvrant entièrement où serait la texture (alpha) de l'overhang natif": faces whose source draw is bch-grassfringe / bch-leafyground-hang-2x1 are flagged (BakeTri bit5 is_hang) and receive TWO decorrelated scatter layers (nspare=7+0.5*layer) rooted at different normal offsets (0.02m / 0.075m), falling straight DOWNWARD with per-layer sway phase+amplitude, ragged tips and an outward belly = visible thickness/depth; length is exit-capped (covers the painted strip, never descends far past it — the round-2 lesson). Inner layer darkened 0.82 for parallax depth. 92928 fall blades (layers=2).
- KILL v5: the rim-drape blade emission is deleted; nothing is placed on dirt/rock drop faces (they are not in the grass texture set); the edge-segment table survives only as zone-1's direction source.
