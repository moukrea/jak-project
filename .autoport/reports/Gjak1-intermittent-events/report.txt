## Named root cause / mechanism (prime suspect, prove-or-disprove mandate)
The named common intermittent mechanism is arm64 bug class #14 stale-icache: jak1_finish's
CacheFlush(base,0) no-op (klink.cpp:623 class) let the arm64 I-fetch serve stale prior-object
instructions at reused KMALLOC_TOP addresses — the exact fingerprint of intermittent, device-only,
random-location trigger failures (a stale instruction stream corrupts whichever :trans/enter-state
behavior happens to relink there). The fix (real-range __builtin___clear_cache over
MAIN/TOP_LEVEL/DEBUG code_infos) landed in Gjak1-icache-flush commit eed5bdd45 and IS in the
deployed build; a runtime revert prop (debug.opengoal.icache.noflush=1, klink.cpp:620-656) restores
the pre-fix no-op-flush behavior for A/B without mixed builds.
A/B verdict, honestly: with the pre-fix noflush behavior restored, NO trigger miss reproduced in 24
trials (see rates below) — so the trials neither reprove nor falsify #14 as the historic cause; what
they DO prove is that with the fix deployed the owner's three symptom classes fire deterministically
at these triggers. The single raw-scored miss (mx-jungle-noflush-c-1, noflush arm) was forensically
a false negative: the raw log shows junglefish-78 idle->patrol->idle via relocation-SPAWN lines
(GOAL heap compaction re-logs a moved proc as SPAWN; the analyzer dropped post-first SPAWNs);
analyzer fixed, all 24 logs re-scored (mx-rescore.txt).
