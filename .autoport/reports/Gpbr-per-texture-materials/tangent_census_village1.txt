-- PER-TEXTURE TANGENT-FRAME CENSUS (Gpbr-per-texture-materials) ---------------------------
   WHAT THIS IS. A SHARE OF FACE CORNERS, never an amplitude: no height field, no camera,
   no tessellation level and no relief slider enters any column below. FRAME: the corner's
   PER-VERTEX tangent frame (T from Gram-Schmidt against the stored normal, B = cross(N,T)
   times sign(.w)) compared against the UV->world Jacobian OF THE FACE (dP/du, dP/dv). A
   corner counts as correct iff dot(T,dP/du) > 0 AND dot(B,dP/dv) > 0. WITH THE DEFECT
   ABSENT BOTH PERCENTAGES READ 100.0000 — that is the value to compare against, not each
   other's history.
   p_nonrep is a property of the ORIGINAL AUTHORED UV LAYOUT: faces of OPPOSITE UV
   handedness meet at one vertex, and .w is one sign per vertex, so no per-vertex tangent
   the format can carry is right for all of them. P_sign EXCLUDES those corners; an
   excluded corner is NOT a correct corner, it is an UNSCORED one. PF_sign scores the same
   corners with the same predicate over the ENTIRE population, and pf_w_wrong is the half
   of that population whose relief actually comes out INVERTED (T right, handedness wrong)
   — the owner's "on dirait que c'est inverse", counted.
