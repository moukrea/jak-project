WHAT CHANGED vs round 3 (design delta committed BEFORE code: design.md)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Root causes of the three round-3 defects were all PER-TRI / PERIODIC structure:
  (a) droop placed as a dense ROOT-EDGE row per tri + 0.28 m LEVEL-SET rows -> the
      diagonal bands; (b) the comb weight came from the FACE normal + a per-tri flag
      (bit4) -> whole triangles flipped state -> the brutal seams; (c) the round-2/3
      transition twin was a straight horizontal chord through the curved lip -> the
      clip-through. Round 4 removes every per-tri field from the visible math:

1. SMOOTH VERTEX NORMALS (GBK5). At bake time the whole retained soup (walkable +
   lip + fringe) is position-welded and each vertex gets the area-weighted average
   of its adjacent face normals; the three per-vertex normals ride in BakeTri
   (vn0/vn1/vn2). expand() interpolates them BARYCENTRICALLY at every blade base, so
   every per-blade quantity is CONTINUOUS across every tri border by construction.
   Computed once on x86, shipped in the .grassbake, read verbatim on device.

2. COMB = per-BLADE CONTINUOUS weight (kills the brutal transition/seam). For each
   walkable blade w = tilt(smooth-normal.y ramp 0.85->0.45) * near(distance to the
   nearest DROOP rim, 1 inside 0.8 m -> 0 at 1.3 m). Both factors are continuous, so
   two adjacent blades can never jump state and no threshold is a line on the mesh.
   Delivered as a toggle-gated TAIL REPLACEMENT twin: the tagged original keeps its
   stock bytes except nspare=-(1+w) (unread when the toggle is OFF), and when ON the
   shader COLLAPSES the tagged original and the twin (carrying the smooth normal +
   w in nspare=5+w) draws the comb. The round-2/3 straight-chord twin class is
   DELETED — the continuous comb field IS the upright->droop transition (crossfade),
   with NO double-up (original collapses, replacement draws; only one blade per base).

3. Droop ROWS -> area-uniform barycentric SCATTER (kills the diagonal bands). Droop
   blades are placed like the walkable lawn (area * density, hashed barycentric) —
   no root-edge row, no level-set rows, nothing periodic. Each blade drapes along
   its OWN smooth-normal in-plane down-slope (continuous over the curl); the round-3
   below-plane vertical sag term is REMOVED.

4. SURFACE CONSTRAINT (kills the clip-through). Each tail blade root is lifted NOFF
   (3 cm) along the smooth normal; the bake plane-caps each blade's rest tip against
   every nearby tri plane (grid hash) — shrinking or dropping it until it clears — and
   the shader half-space-clamps every vertex (incl. sway/trample) to the base tangent
   plane. The bake plane-cap MIRRORS the objective metric's tip formula, so the
   measured tip-violation count is ~0 by construction (not by eyeball).

5. Near LOD = 3D drooping/hang grass (the droop tail, blade pass only). FAR LOD keeps
   the stock painted alpha overhang texture with NO grass cards (the card pass never
   draws the droop/comb tail: card_n stops at droop_start).

6. GBK4 -> GBK5 bump; a v4 bake fails the version check and falls back to live scan.
