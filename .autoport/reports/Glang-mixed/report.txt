French (texte=FR / français), SOME texts remained in English — specifically interaction
prompts with objects or NPCs (personnages). Mandate: they must follow the TEXT language.

## WHERE THE LANGUAGE ID IS LOST (root cause) — Android/arm64 divergence
game/sce/libscf.cpp sceScfGetLanguage(), __linux__ path, reads getenv("LANG").
Android app processes have NO $LANG in their environment -> the function always returns
SCE_ENGLISH_LANGUAGE on device. pckernel-impl.gc:122-123 (reset-misc) seeds
(-> *pc-settings* text-language) AND subtitle-language from (scf-get-language) whenever
pc-settings.gc is absent — fresh install, wiped files/, or a settings reset. So on the
owner's FRENCH phone (persist.sys.locale=fr-FR, verified) every defaults-boot silently
fell back to ENGLISH text: the text-language id is lost between the device locale and
masterConfig.language. The owner's v3 playtest day included exactly such resets (fresh
installs/wipes by the validation pipeline, plus the Rock Village crash-to-home) — after
one, in-game interaction prompts read English while the owner's remembered/re-applied
settings (and separately-keyed content) stayed French, matching "audio EN + some texts EN".

ORACLE VERDICT (pristine x86 golden vs device): sceScfGetLanguage is byte-identical to the
pristine merge-base 704972dd6 (git diff empty), so gold == our-x86 here. Headless x86 runs
with pc-settings.gc wiped (validator-style boot, -fakeiso out/jak1/iso):
  LANG=fr_FR.UTF-8 -> loads 1COMMON.TXT + 1SUBTIT.TXT (FRENCH defaults)   [reached logo]
  LANG=C           -> loads 0COMMON.TXT + 0SUBTIT.TXT (ENGLISH defaults)  [reached logo]
Device defaults-boot (settings wiped, locale fr-FR): loads 0SUBTIT/0COMMON = the LANG-unset
behavior (defaults-title.png). x86 follows the OS language; the Android side did NOT —
a DIVERGENCE in the translation layer (missing locale bridge), fixed below. With settings
present, x86 and device behave identically (text bank keyed off text-language,
text.gc:135, PC_PORT path) — no arm64 codegen divergence found in the text path.

## FIXES (translation layer + pc/ + assets only; engine goal_src untouched)
1. android/app/src/main/java/org/opengoal/gk/MainActivity.java (onCreate, before
   super.onCreate): locale bridge — Os.setenv("LANG", Locale.getDefault().toLanguageTag())
   so the EXISTING desktop __linux__ path in libscf.cpp works 1-to-1 on device. A French
   phone now seeds French text/subtitle defaults; text follows the device language even
   after a settings wipe. No engine change, no libscf change.
2. game/assets/jak1/subtitle/subtitle_lines_fr-FR.json: French translations added for the
   6 base-English scenes (ASSTLP01/ASSTLP31/SAGELP20 "HUM...", MSH-AM08 empty-parity,
   sksp0076, evilbro-misty-end full 8 lines, phrased per the existing FR corpus —
   "VILLAGE DES PIERRES", "ARMÉE DE LURKERS"). Line counts match the EN meta (scene-level
   :lines-base merge verified in common/serialization/subtitles/subtitles_v1.cpp).
3. goal_src/jak1/pc/progress-pc.gc + game_custom_text_{en-US,fr-FR}.json: the six
   Android menu labels become runtime buffers re-formatted from new text ids
   #x1700-#x1705 on every menu open (init-game-options) and immediately after a
   text-language change (refresh-runtime-option-labels!) — they now follow the TEXT
   language; other languages fall back to English via the fallback bank (upstream pattern).
   pc-settings persistence for language changes was verified already-in-place
   (commit-to-file on option confirm, progress-pc.gc).
