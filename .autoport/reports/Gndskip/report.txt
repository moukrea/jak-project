Gndskip — START at the Naughty Dog logo skips TO the Jak and Daxter logo
=========================================================================
RESULT: ND SKIP LANDS ON JAK LOGO

## Symptom (owner, v3 playtest)
Pressing START during the Naughty Dog logo does not skip: the screen goes BLACK
while the ND sequence's audio keeps playing, until the J&D logo appears at its
normal time.

- START injected (cpad_inject 'start', logcat F1D-INJECT button0=0x0008) at
  video t=18.23s, mid ND-logo beat.
- Screen goes black at t=18.0-18.2s; sustained PURE BLACK t=18.2s -> 24.8s.
- J&D logo appears at t=25.0s.
- MEASURED BLACK GAP = 7.0 seconds.
- Natural no-press run (gndskip-natural.mp4): J&D logo also at t~25s — the press

  ndi-intro cutscene clock fnum 21.46 -> 57.88 past the press; NDINTRO STR

  => orphan ND audio confirmed.

## Root cause (skip path)
goal_src/jak1/levels/title/title-obs.gc, ndi :trans.
Upstream (82538625e4): press START/circle/X (gate `(or *debug-segment* PC_PORT)`,
village1 loaded) -> `(set-setting! 'bg-a 'abs 1.0 0)`; the deactivate body
(bg-a==1.0 + village1 loaded + all-visible? != 'loading) then runs
`ja-abort-spooled-anim` (str-play-stop = ND audio killed) + `deactivate self`;
the parent (target-title :code, title-obs.gc:698-703) sees the ndi logo process
die and immediately process-spawns the J&D 'logo beat.
Our Gndlogo fix (dba79ce2da) gated BOTH the village1 display-self AND the
deactivate body's exit condition on `(-> self done?)` (spool finished) to keep
village1 hidden behind the ND logo on the slow Android loader. Side effect:
during the spool done? = #f, so the press only set bg-a=1.0 (black) and the
abort/deactivate body was unreachable until the spool's natural end -> 7.0s
black hole + orphan ndi audio.

## Fix (pc/translation layer scope: TIT.DGO source, same file as Gndlogo)
goal_src/jak1/levels/title/title-obs.gc (ships in TIT.DGO — rebuildable level
DGO; engine goal_src untouched; documented og:autoport markers):
1. New latched flag `*gnd-ndi-skip*` (defined next to the Gndlogo sun-fade
   saves; reset in ndi :enter). The press handler latches it because
   `(-> self anim)` is only assigned once :code sees the spool 'active (zero
   before — process fields are zero-initialized by activate's mem-set32!), so
   an early press must apply on a later frame.
2. New ndi :trans step: `(when (and *gnd-ndi-skip* (not (-> self done?))
   (nonzero? (-> self anim))) (ja-abort-spooled-anim (-> self anim) ...)
   (set! (-> self done?) #t))` — a latched skip is treated as "the spool just
   finished": the abort (str-play-stop) kills the ND audio exactly like the
   pristine body did, and flipping done? lets the already-validated natural-end
   machinery run NOW (done?-gated display-self under the bg-a=1.0 black cover
   -> vis resolves -> deactivate -> parent spawns the J&D logo).
The Gndlogo/Ghalo invariants are preserved: display-self still only ever fires
with done? = #t (village never renders behind the ND logo), and the sun-fade
suppression/restore in :enter/:trans/:exit is untouched.
