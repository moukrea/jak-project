2026-07-10 — HONEST ROOT-CAUSE + FIX (this closes attempt 1's false-green)
====================================================================================
Attempt 1 shipped a report claiming "renders on device" but NOTHING was visible beyond the stock
grass texture. On-device C++ diagnostics (temporary, since removed) proved WHY — two real bugs:

 (1) PLACEMENT / index-slice bug. The per-draw triangle slice length was derived from the COMPRESSED
     runs/plain_indices, which is NOT the expanded index-buffer length. That walked past each draw into
     neighboring vertices and fabricated LEVEL-SPANNING triangles; the 120000-instance cap then scattered
     grass across a 106x96 m region ~100-200 m from the player (bbox y ~14-25 m ELEVATED, all beyond the
     LOD bands) — so 0/120000 blades were near the camera and nothing drew. Fixed by using the
     AUTHORITATIVE slice length = sum(draw.vis_groups[].num_inds) — the exact slice the scene renderer
     uploads (make_all_visible_index_list, background_common.cpp) — plus a spurious-giant-triangle area cap.
     After the fix: maxArea dropped from level-spanning to ~6-8 m^2, giant-skipped=0 (real ground tris only).

 (2) The tra-grass TEXTURE in the training level is on the ELEVATED grassy SLOPES (~127 m from the
     Geyser Rock spawn, on-device enumeration: nearest tra-grass draw at 127-128 m). The WALKABLE GROUND
     the player actually stands on at spawn is textured "tra-beachrock" (the green, mossy Geyser-Rock
     ground) — verified as the nearest walkable ground draw under the camera. tra-grass ALONE never reaches
     the player, so nothing showed up close. Fix: PLACEMENT MECHANISM now matches a curated set of the
     training grassy-ground textures (still 100% texture-driven, no hand-authoring): tra-grass (slopes) +
     tra-beachrock (the green spawn ground) + the bch-grassfringe / bch-leafyground-hang-2x1 fringes.

 (3) Instance budget is now FOCUSED near the player: only ground within 60 m of the camera is populated
     (far grass is past the LOD bands anyway), and the field REBUILDS when the player walks >25 m, so
     dense grass follows the player within the 120000 cap instead of being wasted on distant geometry.

====================================================================================
PLACEMENT MECHANISM — driven by the GROUND TEXTURE (programmatic, no hand-authoring)
====================================================================================
At render time (once per level load, and again when the player moves >25 m), GrassRenderer scans the
training level's loaded tfrag geometry (Loader::get_tfrag3_level("training") -> tfrag3::Level,
highest-detail tfrag_trees[0]). For each StripDraw it reads the bound texture name via
lev->textures[draw.tree_tex_id].debug_name and keeps ONLY draws whose texture is in the curated
grass-ground set (tra-grass / tra-beachrock / bch-grassfringe / bch-leafyground-hang-2x1 — the training
grassy-ground textures per recharged_assets/grass_reference/MANIFEST.md, extended to the green walkable
spawn ground). It reconstructs that draw's triangles from the correct index slice (sum of vis_groups
num_inds; UINT32_MAX strip restart, or plain triple list), filters to walkable GROUND (face-normal +Y
dominant, |ny|/|n| > 0.7), rejects spurious huge tris (>300 m^2), skips tris beyond 60 m of the camera,
and scatters grass RANDOMLY inside each qualifying triangle by barycentric sampling. Placement is
DETERMINISTIC (a 32-bit hash of the triangle indices seeds position/size/yaw/tint/curve/phase), so the
field is stable frame-to-frame (no shimmer). Density is tunable (DENSITY_PER_M2 = 45 tufts/m^2, capped at
120000). Positions sit on the actual triangle plane; vertices are world-space (4096 GOAL units = 1 m),
reused directly with the game camera. Hard-scoped to training only: get_tfrag3_level("training") is null
in every other level, so grass never appears elsewhere.

====================================================================================
INSTANCE / LOD ARCHITECTURE  (renderer-side; engine goal_src untouched)
====================================================================================
One per-instance VBO (world pos + height + yaw/tint/curve/phase), rebuilt on level-load / player move.
Drawn every frame as TWO instanced passes over the same instances (glDrawArraysInstanced, geometry
generated from gl_VertexID — no per-vertex buffer):
  * NEAR tier (u_mode 0): individual 3D BLADES, 10-vert triangle strip.
  * MID  tier (u_mode 1): grass CARDS = X-cross of two crossed flat quads, 12 verts.
  * FAR  tier           : nothing added — the original ground texture only.
LOD is a per-instance distance CROSSFADE in the vertex shader (camera-to-blade distance, world units):
blades fade out ~10->18 m; cards fade in ~9->18 m, hold, fade out ~35->55 m; beyond that the vertex
collapses off-screen (no fragments) so there is no hard pop. Sells the intended illusion: a real lawn up
close, believable filler at mid range, stock game far away.

Hook: drawn at bucket 30 (SHRUB_GENERIC_LEVEL1, i.e. right after all opaque background, depth buffer +
game camera ready). Added in BOTH renderers — desktop game/graphics/opengl_renderer/OpenGLRenderer.cpp AND
the on-device android/android_opengl_renderer.cpp — gated on Gfx::g_global_settings.recharged_grass.
World->clip transform mirrors the scene's GS-style transform (camera matrix + hvdf + fixed-point center),
so grass lands on the exact scene pixels (verified on-device: sampled instances project on-screen with
valid NDC). Depth test GEQUAL (reversed-Z, matches the device's depth clear) + alpha blend.

====================================================================================
BLADE LOOK PARAMETERS (the near tier — the PoC test)
====================================================================================
- FLAT COLOR, NO TEXTURE (per the PoC / couleur plate): grass-green vertical gradient (dark base ->
  bright tip) with per-blade TINT variation (brightness + slight hue), no sampler at all.
- Variable SIZE / scale: per-instance height ~0.14..0.29 m (hash-driven), width tapers to the tip.
- Variable ORIENTATION: per-instance YAW / rotation (0..2pi); width axis rotated by yaw.
- Variable CURVATURE: per-instance bend amount; the blade is a curved 4-segment strip (bent forward as
  t^2 up its height), not a straight quad.

BREEZE (idle wind motion): per-instance PHASE offset + shader time -> each blade sways independently
(sin(time*1.6 + phase), growing toward the tip) — the field is NOT in lockstep. Cards sway gentler.

TRAMPLE (flatten under Jak): Jak's world position is pushed to C++ every frame (pc-set-jak-pos! bridge,
from (-> *target* control trans) in the pc-layer update-to-os). The blade/card shader flattens + bends
grass radially AWAY from Jak within ~2.2 m (height pressed down + tip pushed outward, strongest at Jak,
recovering to the edge). Instant radial flatten with distance falloff (recovery as Jak's uniform moves).
