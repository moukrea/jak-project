OWNER POLISH ROUND 2026-07-10 — what the owner asked, and exactly what changed
====================================================================================
Owner (verbatim, impressed but iterate): full-3D grass rendered close to camera but NOT far enough
for Jak to be IN it (he was in the cards); cards looked like "blurry rectangles" not tufts, didn't
sway in the wind, and sometimes drew as "weird empty squares with nothing in the middle" that didn't
fix by moving around (uncontrolled pop-in); the real 3D grass needed MUCH more density (#1 ask) and
more tint/size variation.

The FIVE fixes (all renderer-side: GrassRenderer.cpp + shaders/grass.vert + shaders/grass.frag —
engine goal_src UNTOUCHED):

1. DENSITY (#1 ask) + JAK STANDS IN THE 3D GRASS.
   - Placement density is now GRADED by camera distance: D_NEAR = 160 tufts/m^2 within 20 m of the
     camera, ramping down to D_FAR = 14 tufts/m^2 by 36 m (density_at() in GrassRenderer.cpp). A thick
     lawn where the player is, a light scatter of cards far away.
   - Triangles are collected then sorted NEAREST-FIRST and scattered in that order, so if the instance
     cap is hit it drops DISTANT cards, never the near blades the player stands in. The cap was raised
     to 220000 (sits above the real total ~179k) so the near lawn is stable.
   - The NEAR-BLADE LOD band was pushed OUT in grass.vert: blades hold fully opaque to 18 m and fade to
     28 m (was 10->18 m); cards only take over from 22 m. Combined with the graded density this puts the
     player firmly INSIDE dense real 3D blades.
   - DEVICE PROOF (logcat, verbatim, Geyser Rock spawn):
       [recharged-grass] training: 10 grass-ground draws, 2433 tris (giant 0, far 12223, maxArea 6m2)
                         -> 178860 instances | cam->jak 10.8m, 135832 in blade-band(28m), 13048 within 8m
     Jak is 10.8 m from the camera — well inside the 18 m full-blade band — with 13048 grass instances
     within 8 m of him (the previous build had 731 in the ENTIRE 18 m band). Density and "Jak in the
     grass" are objectively fixed.

2. MORE TINT / SIZE VARIATION (grass.vert / GrassRenderer.cpp).
   - Blade SIZE (height) range widened to 0.55..1.70x BASE_H (was 0.70..1.45x); CURVATURE 0.10..0.85
     (was 0.15..0.70). Per-blade BRIGHTNESS widened to 0.62..1.34x plus a HUE jitter (a decorrelated
     secondary random shifts each blade warmer<->cooler green). Foreground close-ups show clearly more
     per-blade tint variation than the first PoC.

3. GRASS CARDS READ AS TUFTS, NOT BLURRY RECTANGLES (grass.frag).
   - The mid-range card (an X-cross of two crossed quads) is now procedurally CUT into ~5 vertical
     sub-blades per quad: each sub-blade fills its slot at the base and tapers to a point, with a jagged
     random top edge, and the gaps between blades are DISCARDed. So the card reads as a bushy grass TUFT
     (alpha-cut blade SHAPE), matching the near-blade greens/heights for a seamless transition — no flat
     rectangle.

4. CARDS NOW SWAY IN THE WIND (grass.vert).
   - The whole field shares one travelling wind "gust" (spatial + per-instance phase). The near blades
     sway on it (as before) AND the cards now sway on the SAME gust, gentler and lower frequency, growing
     toward the top. Previously the cards were effectively static.

5. SYSTEMIC "EMPTY SQUARE" / CHUNK DE-INSTANCING POP-IN FIXED — affects BOTH near 3D BLADES and cards.
   OWNER CORRECTION 2026-07-10 (verbatim): "ça concerne aussi l'herbe en vraie 3D, comme si des chunks
   entiers se désinstancient en dépit du fait qu'on soit à proximité... une sorte de culling/pop-in/lod
   system non maîtrisé". i.e. the de-instancing is SYSTEMIC (not card-only): whole CHUNKS of grass drop
   out even when the player is close. This was the #1 correctness bug.
   ROOT CAUSE: the old build's 120k instance cap was HIT and CONSUMED in tfrag DRAW ORDER — so whole
   ground triangles/chunks processed AFTER the budget ran out got ZERO grass even when NEARBY, and which
   chunks won the budget SHIFTED every time the field rebuilt (on player move). That is exactly
   "chunks de-instancing despite proximity" and "only fixes by moving", for BOTH blades and cards. A
   second, card-only contributor: the translucent card rectangle wrote depth, so a crossed quad could
   occlude itself into a hollow "empty square".
   FIX: (a) placement now COLLECTS all qualifying ground triangles, SORTS them NEAREST-FIRST, and fills
   in that order with the cap raised to 220000 — which sits ABOVE the real instance total (~179k), so
   the cap is never hit in practice and, even if it were, the budget is spent on the CLOSEST chunks
   first. No nearby chunk (blade OR card) is ever starved; placement is deterministic and stable
   frame-to-frame (seeded by triangle identity, not draw order or camera). No frustum/chunk visibility
   culling is done in the grass path (the GPU clips off-screen instances), so no chunk-visibility bug
   can drop a nearby chunk. (b) the card tuft cutout DISCARDs the gaps so no translucent rectangle
   writes depth — no hollow square.
   OBJECTIVE EVIDENCE: across the 18 s MOVING walkthrough (36 sampled frames, which INCLUDES field
   rebuilds as Jak walks), the near-ground grass coverage in front of Jak holds a rock-steady
   0.766..0.769 (0.3% variation) — no coverage dips, no bald patches, no chunk disappearing while in
   range. A de-instancing chunk would show as a sharp coverage dip; there is none. (walk_trample_*.png +
   grass_walk_polish.mp4.)

====================================================================================
PLACEMENT MECHANISM — driven by the GROUND TEXTURE (programmatic, no hand-authoring)
====================================================================================
At render time (once per level load, and again when the player moves >20 m), GrassRenderer scans the
training level's loaded tfrag geometry (Loader::get_tfrag3_level("training") -> tfrag3::Level, geo 0).
For each StripDraw it reads the bound texture name (lev->textures[draw.tree_tex_id].debug_name) and keeps
only draws whose texture is in the curated training grassy-ground set: tra-grass (the elevated grassy
slopes), tra-beachrock (the green mossy walkable ground the player stands on at spawn), plus the
bch-grassfringe / bch-leafyground-hang-2x1 fringes (per recharged_assets/grass_reference/MANIFEST.md,
extended to the green spawn ground since tra-grass alone sits ~127 m away). It reconstructs that draw's
triangles from the AUTHORITATIVE index slice (sum of vis_groups num_inds), filters to walkable GROUND
(face-normal +Y dominant), rejects spurious huge triangles, keeps only triangles within 64 m of the
camera, and scatters grass by barycentric sampling. Placement is DETERMINISTIC (a 32-bit hash of the
triangle identity seeds position/size/yaw/tint/curve/phase) so the field is stable frame-to-frame.
Hard-scoped to training only: get_tfrag3_level("training") is null in every other level, so grass never
appears elsewhere.

====================================================================================
INSTANCE / LOD ARCHITECTURE (renderer-side; engine goal_src untouched)
====================================================================================
One per-instance VBO (world pos + height + yaw/tint/curve/phase), rebuilt on level-load / player move.
Drawn every frame as TWO instanced passes over the same instances (glDrawArraysInstanced, geometry from
gl_VertexID — no per-vertex buffer):
  * NEAR tier (u_mode 0): individual 3D BLADES, curved/tapered 10-vert triangle strip. FLAT COLOR (no
    texture, per the PoC) — vertical green gradient (dark base -> bright tip) + per-blade tint/hue.
  * MID  tier (u_mode 1): grass CARDS = X-cross of two crossed quads, procedurally alpha-cut into blade
    tufts in the fragment shader.
  * FAR  tier           : nothing added — the original ground texture only.
LOD is a per-instance distance CROSSFADE in the vertex shader: blades fade 18->28 m; cards fade in
22->32 m, hold, fade out 46->62 m; beyond that the vertex collapses off-screen so no fragments run (no
hard pop). Hooked at bucket 30 (SHRUB_GENERIC_LEVEL1, right after opaque background) in BOTH renderers —
desktop game/graphics/opengl_renderer/OpenGLRenderer.cpp AND on-device android/android_opengl_renderer.cpp
— gated on Gfx::g_global_settings.recharged_grass. World->clip mirrors the scene transform so grass lands
on the exact scene pixels. Depth GEQUAL (reversed-Z) + alpha blend; the tuft cutout uses discard so the
gaps write no depth.

====================================================================================
PERF (Adreno 618, Geyser Rock) — fps cost (honest)
====================================================================================
Grass ON at Geyser holds ~31 fps with the game's dynamic render-scale at its configured 40% floor
(2.48M tris, 178860 instances x 2 instanced passes). Dense grass genuinely works this mid-range GPU: the
dynamic-res system drops to its floor to keep up. This is the honest cost of the owner's "surtout pas
assez dense" density (the previous, sparser PoC sampled ~36 fps). It stays PLAYABLE (crash-free gameplay
throughout). Density is a single-constant lever: D_NEAR / NEAR_DENSE_R in GrassRenderer.cpp trade
density for fps directly; the LOD bands trade blade reach for fps. A clean grass-specific ON-vs-OFF fps
delta could not be isolated cleanly this run (the OFF window was brief + menu-adjacent, and the Redmi is a
shared test device). OWNER PLAY-TEST is the final perf judge; if it feels heavy, lowering D_NEAR is a
one-line change.
