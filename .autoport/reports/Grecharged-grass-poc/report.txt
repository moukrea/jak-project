THE CULLING / DE-INSTANCING BUG — REAL ROOT CAUSE (owner feedback #2, verbatim)
====================================================================================
Owner (verbatim, French): "en bougeant on a des zones entières qui disparaissent, sinon des zones qui
chargent pas, sinon ça fait du pop-in en bougeant... Et on a pas les grass cards à distance." Earlier
correction: "ça concerne aussi l'herbe en vraie 3D, comme si des chunks entiers se désinstancient en
dépit du fait qu'on soit à proximité."

The PRIOR build's placement was CAMERA-RELATIVE and frozen into a buffer that only refreshed in big
jumps. Three coupled mechanisms in GrassRenderer.cpp caused EXACTLY the reported symptoms:
  1. MAX_PLACE_DIST = 64 m CAMERA WINDOW at build time — ground beyond 64 m of the *build-time* camera
     got ZERO grass and stayed empty as you walked toward it   -> "des zones qui chargent pas".
  2. REBUILD_MOVE_DIST = 20 m discrete REBUILD trigger — the static buffer only refreshed after 20 m of
     movement, so a whole new field snapped in at once         -> "pop-in en bougeant" + a CPU hitch.
  3. density_at(camera_distance) GRADING frozen into the buffer — every rebuild re-graded each triangle's
     instance count by the NEW camera distance, so a chunk that was now farther lost its instances
                                                                -> "zones entières qui disparaissent en
                                                                    dépit du fait qu'on soit à proximité".
The report's earlier "nearest-first + cap 220k" change never touched any of these three — that is why
the owner STILL saw de-instancing on the interim build.

====================================================================================
THE FIX — WHOLE-LEVEL, CAMERA-INDEPENDENT STATIC PLACEMENT (no window, no move-rebuild, no re-grade)
====================================================================================
Grass is now placed ONCE at level load over the WHOLE training level, world-space, at a UNIFORM density,
into a STATIC complete instance buffer. It is NEVER rebuilt on camera movement and NEVER re-graded by
camera distance. This kills all three root causes at once:
  * No camera window   -> every qualifying ground triangle is always populated -> nothing fails to load.
  * No move-rebuild     -> the buffer is immutable while walking -> no pop-in, no hitch, no snap.
  * No distance grading -> placement is a pure deterministic function of triangle identity -> no chunk
                           can thin or de-instance; the field is byte-identical frame to frame.
All LOD / visibility is done PER-INSTANCE in the shader from the LIVE camera: near -> 3D blades, mid ->
cards, far -> collapse offscreen. The GPU decides what to draw every frame over the complete static field,
so walking just slides the LOD bands over grass that is ALWAYS there. Density is auto-scaled to an
instance budget so the whole-level field stays bounded regardless of level size (a mid-list cap hit — the
old failure mode — can never happen: expected count is held under the ceiling so no triangle is starved).
Rebuild happens only on a genuine level change/reload (cached load_id).

Placement numbers (device log, verbatim — POLISH#3 build):

  11349 tris kept (giant 0, maxArea 8m2), area 20586 m2, density 28/m2 -> 575989 instances in 389 chunks.
  No camera window, no move-rebuild -> nothing de-instances while moving.
(Was 8803 tris / 17814 m2 / 16 per m2 / 288007 inst / 381 chunks. POLISH#3 raised MAX_INSTANCES
 320k -> 640k so the budget-clamped uniform density ~doubled 16 -> 28/m2; the relaxed walkable-ground
 upness gate (0.7 -> 0.40) added the sloped/bumpy platforms' triangles, so tris/area/chunks rose too.)

====================================================================================
PLACEMENT MECHANISM — driven by the GROUND TEXTURE (tra-grass; programmatic, no hand-authoring)
====================================================================================
At level load GrassRenderer scans the training level's loaded tfrag3 geometry (geo 0). For each StripDraw
it reads the bound texture name (lev->textures[draw.tree_tex_id].debug_name) and keeps only draws whose
texture is in the curated training grassy-ground set: tra-grass (the elevated grassy terrain), tra-beachrock
(the green mossy walkable ground the player stands on at the Geyser Rock spawn), plus the bch-grassfringe /
bch-leafyground-hang-2x1 fringes. It reconstructs that draw's triangles from the AUTHORITATIVE index slice
(sum of the draw's vis_groups num_inds), filters to walkable GROUND (face-normal +Y dominant), rejects
spurious huge triangles, and scatters grass by barycentric sampling. Placement is DETERMINISTIC (a 32-bit
hash of the triangle identity seeds each blade's position/size/yaw/tint/curve/breeze-phase) and CAMERA-
INDEPENDENT, so the field is identical every load and stable frame-to-frame. Hard-scoped to training only:
get_tfrag3_level("training") is null in every other level, so grass never appears elsewhere.

====================================================================================
INSTANCE / LOD ARCHITECTURE (renderer-side; engine goal_src untouched)
====================================================================================
One per-instance VBO (world pos + height, and yaw/tint/curve/breeze-phase), built once per level load.
Drawn every frame as TWO instanced passes over the SAME instances (geometry generated from gl_VertexID):
  * NEAR tier (u_mode 0): individual 3D BLADES — a curved/tapered 10-vertex triangle strip. FLAT COLOR
    (NO texture, per the PoC) — a vertical green gradient (dark base -> bright tip) plus per-blade tint,
    wider brightness variation and a hue jitter (warmer<->cooler green).
  * MID  tier (u_mode 1): grass CARDS — an X-cross of two crossed quads, procedurally alpha-cut in the
    fragment shader into ~5 vertical sub-blade TUFTS (discarding the gaps) so a card reads as a bushy grass
    tuft, not a blurry rectangle.
  * FAR  tier           : nothing added — the original ground TEXTURE only.
LOD is a per-instance distance CROSSFADE in the vertex shader from the LIVE camera: blades hold to 18 m and
fade out by 28 m (so Jak stands IN the real 3D blades — cam->player ~10 m is well inside the band); cards
fade in 22–32 m, hold, fade out 46–62 m; beyond that the vertex collapses offscreen (no fragments, no hard
pop). Blades have variable SIZE (height 0.55..1.70x), variable ORIENTATION (random yaw), and variable
CURVATURE (bent 0.10..0.85), each seeded per instance. Hooked at bucket 30 (right after opaque background)
in BOTH renderers — desktop OpenGLRenderer.cpp AND on-device android_opengl_renderer.cpp — gated on
Gfx::g_global_settings.recharged_grass.

BREEZE (wind idle motion): the whole field shares one travelling wind gust (spatial + per-instance phase),
so it reads as ONE breeze but no two blades move in lockstep. The near blades sway on it (growing toward the
tip), and the mid CARDS now sway on the SAME gust — gentler and lower frequency (owner polish: cards were
static). TRAMPLE: Jak's world position is pushed to the shader every frame (pc-set-jak-pos! bridge); grass
within ~2.2 m of Jak flattens (height pressed down + tips pushed radially away, recovering to the edge).
Best seen in motion in grass_moving.mp4; a disturbed patch is visible at Jak's feet in the stills.

   "bougent beaucoup plus à fond que devant".

4. SLOPED-PLATFORM PLACEMENT (owner: "certaines plateformes ne reçoivent pas d'herbe ... le relief est le
   moins plat ... leur point de référence est plat sur le point le plus bas, les rendant invisible sous le
   sol"). Placement already samples the ACTUAL per-triangle surface (barycentric on the real tri plane —
   never a flat/min-Y reference), so the real cause was the walkable-ground gate: GROUND_UPNESS = 0.7

   lowest) tris — exactly "flat at the lowest point / under the surface". Relaxed the gate 0.7 -> 0.40 so
   sloped/bumpy grass-textured surfaces qualify and get grass placed on their real per-triangle height

5. AIRBORNE TRAMPLE GATE (owner: "quand on est en l'air (en train de sauter), l'herbe en dessous se plie
   comme si on marchait dessus"). The trample now checks Jak's ALTITUDE: vgap = Jak-root-Y - blade-base-Y;
   trample only applies when vgap is in [-1.5 m, +2.0 m] of the grass ground. When Jak jumps and rises
   >2 m above the grass he is airborne, so the grass no longer bends under him. Walking on the ground
   (vgap ~0) still tramples normally.
