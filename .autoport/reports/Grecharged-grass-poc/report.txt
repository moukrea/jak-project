ROOT CAUSE (why round#14 was only partial): the height-taper mechanism is FINE — its INPUT was fragile.
rim_dist was computed from mesh-edge TOPOLOGY (the bAB/bBC/bCA edge-COUNT boundary flags: an edge is a
"rim" iff used by exactly one KEPT grass triangle). On some platforms the topology defeats that:
  * TIE multi-fragment meshes whose vertices do NOT weld across fragments at the 1 cm quantum;
  * non-manifold seams / duplicated tris where a real outer edge is counted more than once;
  * a real drop-off edge SHARED with a mis-classified skirt/overhang-lip tri (so it is not "used once").
There rim_dist stays LARGE right up to the real drop -> the smooth height-taper (round#14) never fires ->
full-height grass at the drop -> the floating the owner still saw. Six rounds of patching the edge-
topology kept leaking because the FOUNDATION (topology-derived edge distance) is fragile.

FIX — a distance-to-edge field computed from GROUND COVERAGE, not triangle edges (GrassRenderer.cpp,
new block right before PHASE 2 placement):
  (1) Rasterize the SOLID walkable TOP into a fine ~0.1 m top-down 2D coverage mask. The silhouette is
      the non-lip grass tris FLATTER than FLOOR_UPNESS = 0.5 (steep overhang skirts are excluded by their
      NORMAL — topology-independent — so an overhang the transitive lip-closure MISSED is still not part
      of the solid silhouette). Cell CENTRES are tested point-in-triangle, so adjacent tris tile gap-free
      (no interior pinholes from shared edges). This is the spec's "clamp coverage to the true solid
      silhouette (the non-lip solid tris)".
  (2) Flood-fill the EXTERIOR = empty cells reachable from the grid border (the void past the platform).
      Interior empty cells — a mid-platform slope surrounded by flat top, an object footprint, a mesh
      pinhole — are NOT exterior, so they never become a rim (no false interior taper / bald ring; the
      round#13 holes stay fixed). A gentle slope BETWEEN two flat tops is an interior hole -> full-height
      grass; only a slope/lip leading to the ACTUAL void tapers.
  (3) Two-pass chamfer distance transform (ortho 1, diag sqrt2; ~2% vs exact, far under the 0.45 m taper)
      seeded at the exterior cells -> rim_dist(x,z) = distance from a base to the nearest exterior cell.
      Stored in gspare and fed to the EXISTING shader height-taper (rim_h = smoothstep(0, 0.45 m,

1. CARDS TOO DENSE/TUFTED -> REDUCE CARD density (owner: "les grass cards sont trop denses et font
   beaucoup plus touffue que la vraie herbe"). Three changes make the cards read LIGHTER than the near

   ground-texture harmonisation + baked-light pipeline, so a card and a blade at the same height are
   the SAME colour BY CONSTRUCTION — no more "l'herbe change de couleur quand on avance". The LOD
   boundary is now a WIDE CROSSFADE: near blades fade out over [0.55, 1.0]*near while cards fade in
   over [0.45, 0.85]*near — the two tiers overlap and BLEND (fade) over the same band, and because
   they are the same colour the transition is SEAMLESS (smooth, no seam/colour jump).

   dessous est plus foncée"). At placement, each ground triangle's BAKED LIGHT is sampled from the
   SAME tfrag/TIE time-of-day colour palette the ground itself is lit by (PackedTimeOfDay, indexed by

   The grass now SITS IN the lighting/brightness instead of floating above it as a flat bright green.
   This is a light-response applied per instance (respond/apply/match the baked shadow/brightness).

====================================================================================
PLACEMENT MECHANISM — driven by the GROUND TEXTURE (tra-grass; programmatic, no hand-authoring)
====================================================================================
At level load GrassRenderer scans the training level's loaded geometry: the tfrag3 ground (tfrag_trees
geo 0) AND the TIE instanced models/platforms (tie_trees geo 0). For each StripDraw it reads the bound
texture name (lev->textures[draw.tree_tex_id].debug_name) and keeps ONLY draws whose texture is a grass
ground texture — the strict per-triangle TEXTURE-ID match (owner "filtrer en fonction de la texture
surtout"): tra-grass, bch-grassfringe, bch-leafyground-hang-2x1. ROCK-textured (and every other
non-grass) face is REJECTED and gets NO grass regardless of normal/slope (the texture filter is the
fix; the surface normal is only a SECONDARY walkable-ground safety net, upness 0.50, rejecting steep/
vertical faces). It reconstructs each draw's triangles from the AUTHORITATIVE index slice (sum of
vis_groups num_inds), and scatters grass by barycentric sampling on the ACTUAL per-triangle surface
(sloped/bumpy platforms get grass at the real per-triangle height, never a flat/min-Y reference).
Placement is DETERMINISTIC (a 32-bit hash of the triangle identity seeds each blade) and
CAMERA-INDEPENDENT. A diagnostic logs any ground-ish texture NOT placed on, so a missed grass variant
surfaces by name (this build's UNMATCHED list is all genuinely non-grass: bch-beach-01,
jng-smallrocks01, vil1-medres-rock, etc.). Hard-scoped to training only: get_tfrag3_level("training")
is null elsewhere. This build's placement line:
  [recharged-grass] training STATIC place (whole-level, camera-independent): 8 grass-ground draws
  (2 TIE), 9300 tris kept, area 19006 m2, density 45/m2 -> 466764 instances in 374 chunks (density
  150% -> budget 960000); occlusion culled 397251 under-object instances (POLISH#6 dilated); bakedRef
  82 (POLISH#6 light-response).

====================================================================================
INSTANCE / LOD ARCHITECTURE (renderer-side; engine goal_src untouched)
====================================================================================
One per-instance VBO (world pos+height; yaw/tint/curve/breeze-phase; ground-colour rgb + baked-light
in w), built once per level load. Drawn every frame as TWO instanced passes over the SAME instances
(geometry from gl_VertexID):
  * NEAR (u_mode 0): individual 3D BLADES — a curved/tapered 10-vertex triangle strip. FLAT COLOR (no
    texture, per the PoC — couleur plate) — a vertical green gradient (dark base -> bright tip) + wide
    per-blade TINT variation (brightness + warm/cool hue jitter), HARMONISED to the ground texture
    colour AND scaled by the per-instance baked light. Variable SIZE (0.50..2.05x), ORIENTATION (yaw),
    CURVATURE (bent 0.10..0.85), breeze phase — each seeded per instance.
  * MID  (u_mode 1): grass CARDS — an X-cross of two crossed quads, alpha-cut in the fragment shader
    into 3 vertical sub-blade TUFTS (gaps discarded, no depth) so a card reads as a grass tuft, not a
    blurry rectangle; ~30% of cards culled + narrower so they are LIGHTER than the near blades; SAME
    colour gradient / tint / ground-harmonise / baked-light as the near blades (seamless).
  * FAR: nothing added — the original ground TEXTURE only.
LOD is a per-instance distance CROSSFADE in the vertex shader from the LIVE camera, using the two
ADJUSTABLE view-distances (Recharged sliders): near blades hold then fade out by u_near_dist (default
30 m); cards fade IN (over a wide band overlapping the blade fade-out -> smooth transition), hold, then
fade out by u_card_dist (default 95 m); beyond that the vertex collapses offscreen (no hard pop). The
grass CARDS render in their mid distance band at range. Hooked at bucket 30 in BOTH renderers (desktop
OpenGLRenderer.cpp AND android_opengl_renderer.cpp), gated on Gfx::g_global_settings.recharged_grass.

BREEZE (wind idle motion): one travelling wind gust (spatial + per-instance phase) so the field reads
as ONE breeze but no two blades move in lockstep. Near blades sway on it (growing toward the tip); the
mid CARDS sway on the SAME gust, GENTLER and lower frequency (card sway <= near-blade sway). TRAMPLE:
Jak's world position is pushed every frame (pc-set-jak-pos!); grass within ~2.2 m FLATTENS (pressed
down + tips pushed radially away), GATED by Jak's ALTITUDE (no bending while airborne/jumping). A SECOND
trample at the ledge-grab point parts the ledge-top grass when Jak hangs.

BAKED-LIGHT DETAIL: bakedRef is the level's area-weighted mean baked luma (this build 82/255). Each
instance's stored baked value is raw_baked/bakedRef clamped [0.45,1.35]; the shader does
col *= mix(1.0, baked, 0.85). Training has real baked variation (mean 82), so shadowed ground darkens
the grass and lit ground keeps it bright — the grass matches the ground brightness/lighting.
