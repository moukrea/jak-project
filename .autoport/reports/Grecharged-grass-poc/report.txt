====================================================================================
PLACEMENT MECHANISM — driven by the GROUND TEXTURE (tra-grass; programmatic, no hand-authoring)
====================================================================================
At level load GrassRenderer scans the training level's loaded geometry: the tfrag3 ground (tfrag_trees
geo 0) AND the TIE instanced models (tie_trees geo 0). For each StripDraw it reads the bound texture name
(lev->textures[draw.tree_tex_id].debug_name) and keeps only draws whose texture is grass ground:
tra-grass (elevated grassy terrain), tra-beachrock (the green mossy walkable ground at the Geyser Rock
spawn), the bch-grassfringe / bch-leafyground fringes, PLUS any texture whose name contains grass/leafy/
moss. It reconstructs that draw's triangles from the AUTHORITATIVE index slice (sum of vis_groups
num_inds), filters to walkable GROUND (face-normal +Y dominant, POLISH#3 upness 0.40 so sloped/bumpy
platforms qualify), rejects spurious huge triangles, and scatters grass by barycentric sampling on the
ACTUAL per-triangle surface (never a flat/min-Y reference). Placement is DETERMINISTIC (a 32-bit hash of
the triangle identity seeds each blade) and CAMERA-INDEPENDENT — the field is identical every load and
stable frame-to-frame. Hard-scoped to training only: get_tfrag3_level("training") is null elsewhere.

====================================================================================
INSTANCE / LOD ARCHITECTURE (renderer-side; engine goal_src untouched)
====================================================================================
One per-instance VBO (world pos+height; yaw/tint/curve/breeze-phase; POLISH#4 ground-colour rgb), built
once per level load. Drawn every frame as TWO instanced passes over the SAME instances (geometry from
gl_VertexID):
  * NEAR (u_mode 0): individual 3D BLADES — a curved/tapered 10-vertex triangle strip. FLAT COLOR (no
    texture, per the PoC) — a vertical green gradient (dark base -> bright tip) + per-blade tint, now
    HARMONISED to the ground texture colour (POLISH#4). Variable SIZE (0.55..1.70x), ORIENTATION (yaw),
    CURVATURE (bent 0.10..0.85), each seeded per instance.
  * MID  (u_mode 1): grass CARDS — an X-cross of two crossed quads, alpha-cut in the fragment shader into
    ~5 vertical sub-blade TUFTS (gaps discarded) so a card reads as a bushy grass tuft, not a blurry
    rectangle. Same greens/height as the near blades (seamless), swaying gentler than the near blades.
  * FAR: nothing added — the original ground TEXTURE only.
LOD is a per-instance distance CROSSFADE in the vertex shader from the LIVE camera, using the two
ADJUSTABLE distances: blades hold then fade out by u_near_dist (default 30 m); cards fade in, hold, then
fade out by u_card_dist (default 95 m, further than before); beyond that the vertex collapses offscreen
(no fragments, no hard pop). Hooked at bucket 30 in BOTH renderers (desktop OpenGLRenderer.cpp AND
android_opengl_renderer.cpp), gated on Gfx::g_global_settings.recharged_grass.

BREEZE (wind idle motion): one travelling wind gust (spatial + per-instance phase) so the field reads as
ONE breeze but no two blades move in lockstep. Near blades sway on it (growing toward the tip); the mid
CARDS sway on the SAME gust, gentler and lower frequency. TRAMPLE: Jak's world position is pushed every
frame (pc-set-jak-pos!); grass within ~2.2 m flattens (pressed down + tips pushed radially away), gated by
Jak's altitude (no bending while airborne). POLISH#4 adds a SECOND trample at the ledge-grab point so the
ledge-top grass parts when Jak hangs.
