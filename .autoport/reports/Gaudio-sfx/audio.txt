Gaudio-sfx — in-game SFX + VOICE reach the AAudio output on device (per-source RMS)
====================================================================================
Device: Redmi Note 9 Pro, ANDROID_SERIAL=eae4df44, package org.opengoal.gk.jak1
Build:  fresh HEAD libgk.so, deploy_verify PASS (build==APK==device).

WHY F2 FALSE-GREENED (the gap this phase fixes)
-----------------------------------------------
The phase-F2 audio gate measured ONE whole-mix PEAK at the AAudio callback
(g_audio_peak in android/android_input_audio.cpp), which is also MONOTONIC (it
keeps the all-time max and never resets). A single whole-mix peak cannot tell
SFX/voice apart from music: "only music plays, SFX+voice silent" still produces
a non-zero whole-mix peak, so it passed as "audio works". That is exactly the
owner-reported false green.

FIX: a per-SOURCE RMS meter in the 989snd synth (game/sound/common/synth.cpp,
game/sound/989snd/audiodiag.h), gated by `setprop debug.opengoal.audio.rms 1`
(env OPENGOAL_AUDIO_RMS=1 on desktop). It splits the synth output by source:
  stream = streamed VAG  (music-stream AND spoken dialog/voice, voices[0..7])
  sfx    = 989snd SBlk sound-bank (BlockSoundVoice) -> the owner's "SFX"
  music  = 989snd SBv2 MusicBank MIDI sequence (midi_handler voices)
Plus a fail-loud guard (game/sound/989snd/loader.cpp): an SFX bank that parses
to ZERO sounds now logs lg::error instead of silently playing nothing — the
exact "music plays (bank-less stream), SFX silent (empty bank)" failure mode.
