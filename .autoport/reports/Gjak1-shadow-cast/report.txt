== ROOT CAUSE (three missing mechanisms, the full Gwater 3-part pattern) ==
1. arm64 mips2c noop: shadow-execute was routed to the real trampoline since Gnd, but its arm64 body was an
   unconditional CURSOR PASS-THROUGH (returned a1 unchanged, geometry never computed) — the Gnd fix only stopped
   the 0-return DMA-cursor collapse, it never ported the geometry. The 15 sibling shadow-* builders were also
   absent from the jak1 kSet allowlist (jak2 had exactly this class; Gjak2-visuals precedent).
2. Android curated renderer subset: ShadowRenderer.cpp + Shadow_PS2.cpp were never compiled into the Android gk
   (android/CMakeLists.txt TU list) — the Gwater lesson (ocean missing) repeated for shadow.
3. Bucket registration: BucketId::SHADOW (47) was listed "unported" -> SkipRenderer in the jak1 Android bucket
   table (android/android_opengl_renderer.cpp init_bucket_renderers_jak1).
