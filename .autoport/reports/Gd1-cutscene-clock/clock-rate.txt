=====================================================================================
WHY THIS IS THE CUTSCENE CLOCK
=====================================================================================
VBlank_Handler advances the stream clock by a RATE-NORMALIZED step:
  gFakeVAGClock += 1024 / target_fps        (srpc.cpp:469-471; target_fps==60, gfx.h:87)
so the clock reaches real-time (1024 units/sec) ONLY when the vblank fires at 60 Hz. The EE
cutscene/scene-player polls info.strpos (= gFakeVAGClock, DMA'd each vblank) to pace every
spooled animation. Thus: cutscene-timeline speed = vblank_Hz / 60.
