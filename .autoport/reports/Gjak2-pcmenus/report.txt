ADDED SCOPE — ORANGE OVERLAY: ROOT-CAUSED + FIXED ON DEVICE
-----------------------------------------------------------
Symptom (owner, twice transferred): a jak1-style translucent ORANGE fill
behind jak2's main/pause menus on device (x86 shows the frozen game world).
Root cause: NOT a menu color and NOT goal_src. jak2's menu backdrop is a
FREEZE-FRAME of the last rendered world frame: at menu-open the GOAL side
(blit-displays.gc:189, fx-copy-buf) emits PC_PORT op 0x10 = "copy the
framebuffer to the pool texture at tbp #x3300", and sky-tng.gc:911 /
sprite-distort.gc:135 then SAMPLE #x3300 to draw the background. On Android,
BlitDisplays (jak2 BUCKET_3 "blit") was SkipRenderer'd (not even compiled
into libgk — android/CMakeLists.txt curates its own TU list), so texture
#x3300 was never populated and the backdrop sampled garbage VRAM = the flat
orange-brown fill. Device A/B: device-jak2-mainmenu.png (BEFORE: ring over
flat orange) vs device-jak2-menu-after.png (AFTER: clean stock dark panel,
orange GONE).
Fix (translation layers only — no goal_src change):
- android/CMakeLists.txt: compile BlitDisplays.cpp + SlowTimeEffect.cpp
  into the Android libgk.
- android/android_opengl_renderer.{h,cpp}: register the REAL BlitDisplays at
  jak2 BUCKET_3 (desktop parity); end-of-frame do_copy_back hook between the
  bucket loop and do_pcrtc_effects (mirrors OpenGLRenderer.cpp:1065); skip
  the frame-start buffer clears when BlitDisplays is registered — desktop
  jak2 deliberately lets BlitDisplays own the clear (OpenGLRenderer.cpp
  "jak 2 does the clear in BlitDisplays.cpp"), and pre-clearing would
  destroy the previous frame before the 0x10 op can capture it. jak1 path
  untouched (m_blit_displays null -> clears exactly as before).
- game/graphics/opengl_renderer/opengl_utils.cpp: FramebufferCopier texture
  GL_RGB -> GL_RGBA under #ifdef __ANDROID__ only (Adreno/GLES blit format
  match; desktop byte-identical).
GL-error scan after the fix: clean (no GL errors, the Adreno blit-format
risk did not fire). Bonus coverage from the same renderer: zoom-blur,
slow-time and menu copy-back effects (0x11/0x13/0x14/0x15) now real on
Android instead of skipped.
