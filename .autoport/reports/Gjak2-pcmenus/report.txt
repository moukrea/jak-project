5. TOUCH NAV (jak1 Gtouch-menus port, adapted to jak2's menu-generic

   pages (all option types: boolean/carousel/slider/link/button/confirm),
   scroll zones (above/below list), off-list = back, the legacy resolution
   picker (two-tap apply) and the aspect-ratio-custom page (column/± zones).
   pc-get-touch-tap rebound from the no-op to the real a35 body for jak2
   (Android + noted in the qemu harness), and the C++ isInMenu tap gate now
   publishes for jak2 (g_overlay_in_menu was jak1-only — new per-frame
   publisher reads jak2's *progress-process* at the __send-gfx-dma-chain
   quiescent point).
   x86-verified end-to-end via menu-touch-simulate! (listener-injected taps
   through the SAME pipeline minus JNI): Dynamic toggle ON/OFF, V-Sync
   ON/OFF, resolution highlight move — x86-touch-toggle.png, x86-listener.txt.
   A REAL defect was found and fixed during this verification: the touch
   confirm never reached respond-to-cpad's post-dispatch selected-option
   block (raw cpad-pressed? only) -> value edits never committed. Fixed by
   ORing the flags there + stale-flag clearing at method top; re-verified.

6. ROOT-CAUSE C++ LAYER (the single biggest jak2-menu fix): on Android jak2
   the FINAL pc-* binding state was the DESKTOP kmachine bodies —
   InitHeapAndSymbol -> InitMachineScheme -> InitMachine_PCPort rebinds the
   whole surface AFTER the a17 no-op pass (order proven in
   game/kernel/jak2/kscheme.cpp:2415-2452). Desktop Display-backed bodies are
   null-guarded no-ops on Android (no desktop Display module) -> 0
   resolutions, dead window-size, os='linux (which is also why the device
   showed the DESKTOP page instead of an Android page). Fix: the 17
   Display/touch/os/fps helpers are re-bound to the a35 android_gfx-truth
   bodies (the exact set jak1 runs) at BOTH points — upgraded in the a17
   early list AND from a new g_jak2_post_machine_scheme_hook fired right
   after InitMachineScheme (mirrors the existing pre-version-check hook;
   null/no-op on desktop + qemu). Desktop filesystem/settings bodies
   (pc-filepath-exists? etc.) are correct on Android and stay.
