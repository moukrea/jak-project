== GROUND-CONTACT FOOTPRINT, NOT THE FULL/BURIED MESH (owner nuance) ==
The cull keys on where the object CLIPS THE GROUND, never on the full mesh or bounding volume:
- TIE occluder points are only accepted in the round#13 contact band [0.05 .. 1.00 m ABOVE local
  ground] — geometry below the ground surface (buried bases) contributes NO occluder points.
- Actor CULL radii are ground footprints, not mesh extents: draw-bounds bsphere-w * 0.8 with the
  owner-validated static factor x1.5, clamped 0.9..3.0 m (GrassRenderer.cpp goal_add); TRAMPLE radii
  come from the collide-shape ROOT-PRIM (the physics ground footprint, "feet not body"), clamped
  0.3..1.2 m.
- The shader occlusion test is Y-banded per blade (OC_STEP: -1.2 m .. +1.0 m around the occluder Y),
  so an occluder can never bald grass on terraces above/below it, and under-ground model volume is
  irrelevant by construction.

with substantial geometry below the walkable surface, yet its cull is the tight r2.70 m ring only —
fresh frame gclip_vent_016/026 shows dense grass right up to the crater rim, plate clean. Same for
the button: gclip_btn2_024 shows a tight clean ring around the r1.80 plate, tall grass at ~1 m.
