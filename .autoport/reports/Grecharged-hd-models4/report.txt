money-lod2) carries adgif texture-id page=0x1cf idx=0x2c. Every companion spawn (login ag) re-links
that shader into *texture-page-dir* entry 0x1cf; HD companion ags live on the GLOBAL heap (loado),

crash registers carry 0x1cf/0x2c verbatim on both platforms.
