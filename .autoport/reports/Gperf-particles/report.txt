   Kill switch: debug.opengoal.perf.notodskip=1 restores the always-run path.
 * SPARTICLE 2d NEON/vectorized fast-path: replace the ~20 mips2c VU0 member-ops of
   the per-particle aging inner loop (sp_process_block_2d block_18) + the s4+24
   frame/color quantization with bit-exact inlined float math (this TU built
   -ffp-contract=off, like the Gcollision-arm VU0 TUs, so multiply-then-add stays
   two-rounding = identical). Cuts the DOMINANT 2d-builder CPU. Kill switch:
   debug.opengoal.perf.no2dvec=1.
Round-1..3 (prior, still active + reversible): GOAL/GL frame OVERLAP (the largest
