== Root cause of round 2 (verified in code, matches the owner's 22:05 correction) ==
The "wall drape" was NOT the droop blades: the lip classifier only considers tris with upness < 0.55,
so the curved TRANSITION BAND (flat-grass-textured curl tris, upness 0.55..0.95, wrapping from the
walkable top down over the lip) stayed classified WALKABLE and grew FULL-HEIGHT UPRIGHT blades whose
bases sit below the visual lip — from the side they read as grass growing out of the wall under the
floor. Round-2 droop itself was a parametric arc (u_droop_len world-scale, length from the face's
vertical span) tied to nothing on the host tri -> ignored the fringe mesh relief and overshot the
painted texture. Tuning could never fix either; round 3 is the mandated structural redesign.

== The mandated design, as implemented ==
1. PLACEMENT BASIS = the fringe/lip tris THEMSELVES: droop blades are placed ON the droop faces
   (fringe-textured bch-grassfringe / bch-leafyground-hang-2x1 tris + the excluded lip tris — all
   grass-textured by the allowlist, never bare wall faces), rooted on each tri's UP-SLOPE edge (where
   the face abuts the walkable rim from the global rim hash, that IS the shared rim edge) and grown
   ALONG the tri plane (in-plane down-slope carried per-instance) -> the droop follows the mesh and
   its relief BY CONSTRUCTION, hanging downward over the drop. Tall faces are tiled with rows
   (ROW_STEP 0.28m, max 6) so multi-row drape meshes are covered row by row.
2. LENGTH FROM THE TRI EXTENT, PER BLADE: length = min(species sample, 0.95 * exact in-plane exit
   distance) -> a blade can NEVER extend past its host tri, i.e. never longer than the fringe texture
   it covers. No global world-space drop constant (u_droop_len survives only as a live debug
   multiplier, default 1.0 neutral; prop debug.opengoal.grass.droop_len).
3. SAME SPECIES: the droop height sample is the walkable formula BASE_H*(0.50+1.55*rand) and the
   standard width formula — the same grass as the adjacent platform blades, bending over the edge.
4. TRANSITION BAND (owner correction): scan flags curl tris (upness < 0.85 AND centroid within 1.5m of
   a droop-rim segment — the global rim hash subset) as bit4; 921 tris / 546 m2 on training. Their
   base blades keep byte-identical placement but carry a NEGATIVE nspare comb weight; when the toggle
   is ON the shader lies them along their own face's in-plane down-slope (tilt-weighted), so the curl
   grass hugs the surface instead of towering out of the wall. NO upright full-height blades remain on
   the band with the toggle ON. Device census proves the tag live: comb_tagged=2048.
5. CONTINUOUS GRADIENT: upright (flat top, LOCKED rim taper) -> transition twins (kept, target re-aimed
   at the comb shape: lean outward toward horizontal, no below-base drop) -> band comb (weight grows
   with tilt) -> rooted droop rows on the fringe mesh. One species, one continuous downward-over-the-lip
   arc; side-sweep capture below.
6. FRINGE TEXTURE: the round-2 near-hide is KEPT verbatim (owner confirmed it works) — u_fringe_fade on
   the same two hang textures, steepness-gated; near-3D and the far texture crossfade over the same
   band, no double-up, no seam. Far LOD unchanged: at distance the ORIGINAL painted texture shows and
   there is no grass card on the lip (droop/twins still collapse in the card pass).
