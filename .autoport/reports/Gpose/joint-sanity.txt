GPOSE JOINT-SANITY TRIPWIRE — objective pose-blink metric (NaN bone-matrix glitch frames)
=========================================================================================
RESULT: before=7416  after=0     (glitch frames eliminated)

Metric definition
-----------------
A "glitch frame" is a rendered cinematic frame during which the arm64 joint
pipeline produced >=1 NON-FINITE (NaN/inf) character bone matrix that would be
skinned by the merc renderer (the literal "pose blink": the skeleton glitches
into a garbage pose, then reverts). Measured on-device, arm64 (eae4df44), during
the NEW-GAME intro cinematic driven headlessly via cpad_inject.

The tripwire lives in game/mips2c/jak1_functions/joint.cpp (the arm64
cspace<-parented-transformq-joint! body, the per-joint bone-matrix builder) and
is armed with debug.opengoal.gpose.tripwire=1 (set by .autoport/Gpose_run.sh):
 - GPOSE-BADMAT  : a bone matrix written by this body came out non-finite/absurd.
 - GPOSE-NORMNAN : a quaternion emitted by normalize_frame_quaternions was NaN.
 - GPOSE-GLITCH frame=N badmat=K : per-rendered-frame bucket (frame index from
                  android_gfx.cpp st.frame_idx). glitch_frames counts frames
                  with badmat>0 (i.e. NaN reaching the renderer, AFTER the fix's
                  repair — so a non-zero value means NaN still escapes).
