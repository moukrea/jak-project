--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. WHAT IS A "NEAR CHUNK" AND ITS DETAILED DRAW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The jak1 ocean is split across two buckets:
  - ocean-mid-and-far  (OceanMidAndFar renderer): the low/medium-detail water out
    to the horizon. Always drawn over water.
  - ocean-near         (OceanNear renderer): the HIGH-DETAIL tessellated water in
    the immediate camera vicinity. Gated in GOAL (ocean.gc:498) by
        (not (or *ocean-near-off* *ocean-mid-off*
                 (< 196608.0 (fabs (-> *math-camera* trans y)))))
    i.e. only when the camera is low (|Y| <= 196608 ~= 48m) over the ocean.

draw-ocean-near (ocean-near.gc:503) loops over a near grid of cells
(near-minx..near-maxx x near-minz..near-maxz) and, per visible cell, emits
ocean-near-add-upload + (ocean-near-add-call 39) — ONE VU "call 39" per near chunk.
OceanNear::render runs the VU2C (run_call0/run_call39 in OceanNear_PS2.cpp),
xgkicks the geometry to CommonOceanRenderer::kick_from_near, and flush_near draws
3 passes (RGB texture / ALPHA / ENVMAP) of the tessellated near mesh.

Per-near-chunk LOD/draw metric (the thing that reflects the defect):
  - call39  = number of near chunks submitted that frame (LOD/extent of near draw)
  - flush_near verts / idx0,idx1,idx2 = detailed near geometry actually drawn
  - "flat/0" near (the blue-squares signature) => call39=0 / flush_near verts=0
    (mid/far flat fallback shows through near the camera).

Instrumentation was TEMPORARY C++ (marked // GWLOD-TEMP) in the translation layer
(OceanNear.cpp, CommonOceanRenderer.cpp) only — NO goal_src edits — and prints the
SAME [GWLOD] lines on x86 (stdout) and device (logcat). It has been REMOVED (see
Gwater-lod-fix-summary.md). Raw logs: x86-gwlod-b.log, dev-after-logcat.log,
dev-before-logcat.log under this directory.
