1. WHY THE MOTION DIED (and the fix). The cycle-13 perimeter rebuild puts a grazing contact
   on every inward half-cycle of a chest or hair chain — their volumes are capped AT the model
   pose, so the model pose sits ON the surface — and the C13d "contacts spend velocity" fix
   then discharged the FULL approach velocity on every one of those grazes, plus contact
   friction took another `fric` of the WHOLE velocity vector on the same frames. Half the
   oscillation died per contact: a sim that satisfied every one-sided calm ceiling by being
   dead. Fixes, solver-side, no data change:
     * the inelastic discharge is now SUSTAINED-CONTACT GATED (all five sites: substep sweep,
       final sweep, cross-side pass, both strand-pass partners, mesh sweep): a chain whose
       stick counter says it is FIGHTING a collider spends everything and goes quiet exactly
       as before; a transient graze spends only PHYS-SPEND-SOFT=0.35 of its approach and keeps
       its swing. The chest-vs-chest "entre-choc" the owner asked for reads as a bounce again.
     * contact friction is TANGENTIAL-ONLY: it used to scale the whole velocity vector, which
       double-charged every graze (the normal component is the discharge's job); its own
       meaning — stop a chain sliding along a surface — is the tangential part, now the only
       part it touches.

3b. HOW THE RESIDUAL IS CLEARED — REWRITTEN (C14c), because the first shape did not converge.
   The previous resolve pushed the LINK out along the worst volume's normal, per SAMPLE, up to
   three times, bounded per push. On the 09:46 device run it bought 5%: jak-hd ended a frame at
   meshpen=475.15 out of mraw=502.55, and dax-hd sat at 537.5458 in two different legs — the
   same value twice, i.e. a fixed configuration it could never climb out of. Two structural
   reasons, neither of them tuning:
     * a push out of volume A lands the sample inside neighbouring volume B and the next
       iteration pushes it back into A. The normals disagree; the iteration orbits instead of
       converging. This is also why meshpen could read HIGHER than mraw (537 > 502): the
       resolve was making some samples worse, which is precisely "une resolution plus genante
       que le clipping".
     * the samples of one link fight each other. Each shoves the shared link origin, so the
       sample resolved first is silently un-resolved by the one resolved last — and only the
       last one's residual was ever re-measured.
   Both disappear if the search is made ONE-DIMENSIONAL and aimed at a known-feasible end. The
   authored pose is that end: with the link origin at *phys-targ*, every sample's test point
   and its own authored floor point are THE SAME POINT, so the effective penetration is exactly
   0 against every body volume simultaneously. The resolve now bisects the segment
   [authored pose -> simulated pose] (6 steps) for the LARGEST fraction of the physics
   deviation that is still clear, and commits that, velocity-neutrally (prev travels with the
   correction, rule O). Properties by construction rather than by hope:
     * it always terminates and always finds a feasible commit — t=0 is feasible by definition;
     * it never overshoots into a neighbouring volume, because it only ever moves TOWARD the
       authored pose and never past it;
     * it keeps every part of the motion that was not the problem — a chest jiggling outward is
       untouched, only travel that goes INTO the body is trimmed;
     * meshpen <= tolerance is therefore a GUARANTEE, not an outcome, which is what "ne doivent
       pas passer au travers du mesh de leur personnage, QU'IMPORTE LA RAISON" actually asks for.
   It is also cheaper: 6 probes replace up to 15 push-and-re-audit rounds per link.
   What this changes about the positive control is stated in its own section below, not hidden.

4. RESOLUTION SMOOTHNESS (C14-D). "Un truc tente d'empecher les meches de clipper dans ses
   oreilles mais ca fait des trucs BIZARRES plus genants qu'un simple clipping." A resolution
   the eye reads as a jump is worse than the clip it replaced. `resjerk` = the worst
   single-frame NET resolution displacement any link took — the length of the VECTOR sum of
   every correction every pass applied to it that frame (substep sweep, final sweep,
   cross-side, model fallback, strand pass, mesh sweep), reported per window and bounded on
   every leg.
   INSTRUMENT CHANGE, STATED OPENLY — THE SECOND ONE, and this time the metric is refuted by
   its own arithmetic rather than by taste. History first, because it matters: the ledger began
   as a sum of correction MAGNITUDES and a settled, visually still link read resjerk=100853 on
   a window whose every calm gate was green. That was corrected to a sum of correction VECTORS,
   on the reasoning that opposing corrections would then cancel. They do not, and the 09:46 run
   shows why in one line — jak-hd's worst window read

       resjerk = 66389.27   while   maxdev = 1031.47

   a claimed 16-metre single-frame jump by a bone that never left a 25 cm neighbourhood of its
   own authored pose all window. The vector sum cannot cancel because the force that UNDOES a
   correction is the SPRING, and the spring never writes to the ledger: the sum only ever sees
   one side of the argument, so it grows monotonically while the bone goes nowhere. It was also
   blind in the other direction — the model-pose ladder, the escape pass and the length restore
   all move out/pos/prev and never touched it at all.
   The declaration always stated the right definition ("where the bone ENDED the frame relative
   to where resolution found it"); it was never what the code computed. It is now: resjerk is
   the worst single-frame change in a link's OFFSET FROM ITS AUTHORED POSE. Differencing
   against the authored pose divides out the character's own travel — an actor crossing 4032
   units in a frame carries his hair with him, and that is not a snap — and leaves exactly the
   artifact the owner described: the physics offset changing discontinuously. A settled link
   holds its offset and reads ~0; an honest swing reads its own per-frame travel (maxvel, ~117
   on that same window); a real one-frame jolt reads its full visible size. It is strictly MORE
   complete than the ledger (it sees every pass, including the three that never wrote to it),
   and it cannot be satisfied by killing the sim, because the locomotion FLOORS are graded on
   this same execution. The superseded ledger is still computed and still printed as `respath=`
   on every window, so the swap is auditable instead of silent; it is not gated.

MESH-LEVEL POSITIVE CONTROL (C14-B — the rule that every zero must be shown able to rise)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
inject= (chain param 26) deliberately displaces every chain INTO the body, data-only, external
override, device file byte-restored. The mesh audit must see the deliberate penetration: mraw
(pre-resolve depth beyond the authored floor) must CLIMB, and mfix (clamps committed) must be
nonzero. The DISARMED run must read meshpen 0 over a nonzero meshtested.

ONE ASSERTION IN THIS CONTROL CHANGED THIS CYCLE, AND IT IS A RELAXATION, SO IT IS FLAGGED
RATHER THAN SLIPPED IN. It used to also demand meshpen > 0 while ARMED, on the correct
principle that a counter which can never rise proves nothing when it reads zero. With the
feasibility clamp of 3b, meshpen CANNOT be nonzero unless the guarantee itself is broken —
demanding that it rise is now demanding that the fix not work. So meshpen stops being the
DETECTOR and becomes a CHECKED POSTCONDITION, and the detection sits on the two counters
either side of the resolve, both still gated and both unchanged: mraw must climb, mfix must
fire. The anti-vacuous-zero rule is met in its own terms — meshpen=0 is not "nobody looked",
it is "mfix commits were each re-audited AT the committed point and every one came back
clear", and mfix is that backing count; a zero with mfix=0 is still an empty zero and still
fails. What the control can no longer prove is that a BROKEN clamp would be caught by
injection: it would be caught by the same re-audit that produces meshpen, but not by this
needle. Stated, not papered over.
