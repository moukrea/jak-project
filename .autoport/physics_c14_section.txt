Three defects, three mechanisms, all three addressed at the mechanism rather than the number:

1. WHY THE MOTION DIED (and the fix). The cycle-13 perimeter rebuild puts a grazing contact
   on every inward half-cycle of a chest or hair chain — their volumes are capped AT the model
   pose, so the model pose sits ON the surface — and the C13d "contacts spend velocity" fix
   then discharged the FULL approach velocity on every one of those grazes, plus contact
   friction took another `fric` of the WHOLE velocity vector on the same frames. Half the
   oscillation died per contact: a sim that satisfied every one-sided calm ceiling by being
   dead. Fixes, solver-side, no data change:
     * the inelastic discharge is now SUSTAINED-CONTACT GATED (all five sites: substep sweep,
       final sweep, cross-side pass, both strand-pass partners, mesh sweep): a chain whose
       stick counter says it is FIGHTING a collider spends everything and goes quiet exactly
       as before; a transient graze spends only PHYS-SPEND-SOFT=0.35 of its approach and keeps
       its swing. The chest-vs-chest "entre-choc" the owner asked for reads as a bounce again.
     * contact friction is TANGENTIAL-ONLY: it used to scale the whole velocity vector, which
       double-charged every graze (the normal component is the discharge's job); its own
       meaning — stop a chain sliding along a surface — is the tangential part, now the only
       part it touches.
   None of the calm machinery moved: rest state, sleep, ring-down and arrival are untouched
   and all idle-gated, so the calm ceilings are defended by the same code as before.
