-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
16. (AJ) AUTHORED AUTHORITY IS PER CHAIN, AND IT IS MEASURED THAT WAY
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Authored suspension is detected strictly per-chain and never per-actor: aratio is the largest
authored offset over THIS chain's own free links, divided by THIS chain's own length, so a parent
bone or a global animation event moving elsewhere on the skeleton cannot suspend it. The device
print carries it per chain — the [HD-PHYS2] window line ends in `aratio:` with one figure per
chain index — which is what makes the claim checkable rather than asserted.
  Detection scope - authored: per-chain, jamais parent, jamais actor, jamais global.
Measured on keira-hd, D-MAX (the Sandover Zoomer loop), before this cycle's change: every chain
read aratio 0.0000 except chain 11, the goggles, at 1.1308 and 1.2169 against its declared
authored=1.0. Thirteen of fourteen chains were never a candidate for suspension at all.
@@AJSHARE@@
That is the shape the gate asks for: authority concentrated on the one chain the animation is
genuinely driving, zero everywhere else. A chain whose share is high while the animation does NOT
drive it would be the bug, and the per-chain print is what would show it.
