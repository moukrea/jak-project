18. (AJ) AUTHORED AUTHORITY, MEASURED PER CHAIN ON THE PHONE — AND ONE CHAIN THAT WAS WRONG
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Owner, 2026-08-07 11:55: "J'ai l'impression que la physique est souvent DESACTIVEE sur les
animations, comme si des BONES NON LIES qui bougent stoppaient les bones/elements avec physique."
That is a testable claim about a share of frames, so it was tested rather than argued.

THE AUDIT.  Only chains that declare `authored=` are candidates for suspension at all — five of
the 502 declared chains do.  For each, the device print carries `aratio` per chain index, so the
share of window frames spent above its own threshold is readable directly off the log.  Measured
across the D-MAX and D-INTRO legs:

  share  model / chain          authored=   aratio p50   aratio max
  93.3%  keira-hd  goggles         1.00        1.280        1.311
  20.0%  dax-hd    earR            0.35        0.178        0.715
  20.0%  dax-hd    earL            0.35        0.193        0.715
   0.0%  jak-hd    earR            0.35        0.004        0.251
   0.0%  jak-hd    earL            0.35        0.004        0.206

Two readings, and they point opposite ways.  Keira's goggles at 93.3% are the intended shape: they
are strapped to her head, their authored pose is permanently far from where gravity would hang
them, and the owner's own complaint (AF) was that they were handed back too EARLY.  Jak's ears at
0.0% show the detector is not firing on actor motion — he walks, runs and turns throughout that
leg and his ears never cross their threshold, which is the direct refutation of "unrelated bones
moving suspend the physics".

DAXTER'S EARS WERE THE REAL CASE, AND IT WAS THE OWNER'S BUG.  Their threshold was 0.35 while
their ordinary idle band measures p50 0.19 and max 0.715 — so one frame in five of ORDINARY
animation handed the animator authority over chains the animation was not deliberately driving.
The cost was visible in the fidelity numbers: dax-hd earR and earL were the run's two worst
model-pose deviations at 5.2477 and 5.1848 units, while EVERY other family A chain on the phone
read at or under 1.1155.  A chain that is repeatedly frozen onto an idle pose never comes home to
the model shape, which is the cycle-5 rule it was failing.

THE CHANGE, AND WHY THIS NUMBER.  Daxter earL/earR `authored=` 0.35 -> 0.80: above the measured
idle band (max 0.715) with margin, and far below what a deliberate, hand-keyed ear gesture reaches.
Nothing is lost from Naughty Dog's animation by this: those chains carry `anim=excite excite=0.6`,
so the authored channel keeps driving them as an INPUT FORCE at all times; what stops is the chain
being pinned onto the idle pose.  The threshold is the only thing that moved — the detector itself
is unchanged, and it remains armed on Daxter exactly as gate Q requires.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED.  This is a five-chain audit because only five chains arm the mechanism.  It
does not and cannot show that a chain with no `authored=` key was ever suspended — such a chain has
no suspension path in the solver at all, which is the stronger statement and is a property of the
code, not of this run.
