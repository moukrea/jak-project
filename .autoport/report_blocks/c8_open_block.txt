-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
19. THE PENETRATION BLOCKER — ONE CAUSE FIXED FROM ITS OWN DECAY RATE, THREE NAMED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The owner has twice been shown a build that looked identical to the one before it, and both times
the reason was that a counter measured something other than what he was looking at.  So every
finding below carries the number that names it, including the ones this cycle does not fix.

(1) THE FINAL RESOLVE DID NOT CONVERGE UNDER VIOLENT MOTION — FIXED, AND THE PHONE SET THE SIZE.
The resolve is Gauss-Seidel over the volumes, so what is left after n sweeps decays geometrically,
and the intro leg gives the rate directly.  Maia's `backhairM` was driven 967.1 units into her own
body during her flight (her window carries anchmove = 628129 units and maxvel = 864 units a frame)
and six sweeps clawed it back to 13.5 — a factor of 0.53 per sweep.  Six more sweeps take that to
about a quarter of a unit, i.e. under the 1.0-unit tolerance, which is exactly the difference
between the leg reporting her clean and reporting her not.  Jak's `collarL` is the same shape at
16.2 units in 4 of its 14 windows.
The sweep count is now derived from the level's own `iters` — `max(6, min(12, iters-4))` — instead
of being a hardcoded 6.  That puts it on the PRECISION SCALE the owner specified rather than
making everyone pay for it: level 0 (iters 2) and level 1 (iters 6) both still run exactly 6
sweeps and are bit-identical to the previous build, and only the top level, which is the one meant
to "coûter beaucoup plus mais être plus précis", goes to 12.  It also stays tunable from the data
file with no build, like the rest of this system.

(2) THE COLLISION CORRECTION IS BOUNDED BY THE CHAIN'S LENGTH, NOT BY ITS ANGULAR LIMIT — NAMED.
The per-correction cap is `maxcorr = max(8, 0.5 * chainlen)`.  On jak-hd, measured on the baked
model:

  chain      link length   angular limit   cone travel bound   maxcorr    measured deviation
  collarL      1220.8 u       12 deg           258.9 u         610.4 u        up to 625.9 u
  shirtL        811.0 u       12 deg           172.0 u         405.5 u        (opposite-leg site)

The collar's own angular limit permits 258.9 units of travel; the resolve is allowed to displace it
by 610.4, and on the phone it reached 625.9 — 2.4x past its own limit — because the cone clamp runs
BEFORE the collider push inside each iteration and is never re-applied after the last one.  The
chain ends the frame wherever the colliders left it.  That is a quantitative account of the owner's
site 1 ("le COL clippe avec ses EPAULES") and site 3 (the jacket pendants crossing into the
opposite leg), and it explains why tuning the collar's stiffness, damping and angle across four
cycles changed nothing: none of those parameters is what places it.  Not changed here, because
tightening the cap trades against the very convergence that (1) just bought, and the two must be
measured one at a time — the method the supervisor imposed after the cycle-5 oscillations.

(3) THE PER-CHAIN RESIDUAL COUNTER `cres` CANNOT REPORT — NAMED.
It is incremented on a residual frame and then zeroed in the `else` branch of the sticking test,
which runs on the next clean frame, so with 2 to 8 residual frames inside a 1500-frame window it is
always zero by the time the window prints.  Every `cres:` array in every log of this phase reads 0,
including on the windows that DID hold a penetration.  Both offending chains above had to be
identified instead from `cclr`, the minimum-clearance accumulator, which is reset only at print
time and whose worst entry equals `-residmax` exactly in all four of jak-hd's failing windows.
This is not a false green — the slot-wide `resid` counter reported the penetrations correctly and
the leg failed the run on them — but the array whose entire purpose is to NAME the chain has never
worked, which is why four cycles of penetration reports say "somewhere on jak-hd" instead of
"collarL".

(4) CHAIN-SIDE COLLISION IS A POINT PER LINK, SO A LONG STRAND HAS GAPS — NAMED.
evilsis-lod0's `backhairM` is 5 links spanning 7486.8 units (1.8 m), with link lengths of 572,
1680, 1234, 1919 and 2081 units, and each link carries a single 260-unit sphere at its joint.
Between two consecutive spheres there are therefore up to ~1200 units — 29 cm — of strand tested
against nothing.  `extent=` does not close this: it only moves the LAST link's test point onto the
rigidly-skinned continuation below the final bone.  This is the same class of hole as the owner's
"capsules qui laissent des interstices", moved from the body onto the hair, and closing it means
testing the SEGMENT between consecutive links rather than the point — a real change to the sweep,
not a parameter, and therefore not something to land in the same cycle as (1).
