-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
19. TWO ROOT CAUSES FOUND THIS CYCLE AND NOT FIXED IN IT — WITH THE NUMBERS THAT NAME THEM
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The owner has said twice that a build looked identical to the one before it.  Both times the
reason turned out to be that the counters were measuring something other than what he was looking
at.  So the two findings below are stated with their numbers even though neither is fixed here:
they are the first quantitative account of two defects that have survived every cycle, and hiding
them until they are fixed is how a cycle ends up looking identical again.

(1) THE COLLISION CORRECTION IS BOUNDED BY THE CHAIN'S LENGTH, NOT BY ITS ANGULAR LIMIT.
The per-correction cap is `maxcorr = max(8, 0.5 * chainlen)`.  On jak-hd, measured on the baked
model, that gives:

  chain      link length   angular limit   cone travel bound   maxcorr    measured deviation
  collarL      1220.8 u       12 deg           258.9 u         610.4 u        up to 625.9 u
  shirtL        811.0 u       12 deg           172.0 u         405.5 u        (opposite-leg site)

The collar's own angular limit permits 258.9 units of travel.  The collision resolve is allowed to
displace it by 610.4, and on the phone it reached 625.9 — 2.4x past its own limit — because the
cone clamp runs BEFORE the collider push inside each iteration and is never re-applied after the
last one.  The chain therefore ends the frame wherever the colliders left it.  That is a
quantitative account of the owner's site 1 ("le COL clippe avec ses EPAULES") and site 3 (the
jacket pendants crossing into the opposite leg), and it explains why tuning the collar's stiffness,
damping and angle across four cycles changed nothing: none of those parameters is what places it.
On the same run collarL is also the only chain whose clearance crossed the tolerance floor, in 4 of
14 windows, by at most 16.2 units (3.9 mm at the file's own scale of 1 unit = 0.24 mm).

(2) THE PER-CHAIN RESIDUAL COUNTER `cres` CANNOT REPORT.  It is incremented on a residual frame and
then zeroed in the `else` branch of the sticking test, which runs on the next clean frame — so with
2 to 8 residual frames inside a 1500-frame window it is always zero by the time the window prints.
Every `cres:` array in every log of this phase reads 0, including on the windows that DID hold a
penetration.  The offending chain had to be identified instead from `cclr`, the minimum-clearance
accumulator, which is reset only at print time and whose worst entry equals `-residmax` exactly in
all four failing windows.  This is not a false green — the slot-wide `resid` counter reported the
penetration correctly and the leg failed the run on it — but the array whose entire purpose is to
NAME the chain has never worked, which is why four cycles of penetration reports say "somewhere on
jak-hd" instead of "collarL".

(3) MAIA'S HAIR: THE COLLISION REPRESENTATION HAS GAPS BY CONSTRUCTION.  evilsis-lod0's `backhairM`
is 5 links spanning 7486.8 units (1.8 m), with link lengths of 572, 1680, 1234, 1919 and 2081
units, and each link carries a single 260-unit sphere at its joint.  Between two consecutive
spheres there are therefore up to ~1200 units — 29 cm — of strand that is not tested against
anything.  `extent=` does not close this: it only moves the LAST link's test point onto the
rigidly-skinned continuation below the final bone.  On the intro leg backhairM is the one chain of
her ten whose clearance crossed the floor, and her window carries anchmove = 628129 units and
maxvel = 864 units per frame — her flight, where the strand is driven 967 units deep before the
resolve claws it back to 13.5.  Chain-side collision is a point per link; on a strand this long
that is the same class of hole as the owner's "capsules qui laissent des interstices", moved from
the body to the hair.
