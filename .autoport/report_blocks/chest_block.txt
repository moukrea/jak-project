
  WHY THE CHEST HAD STOPPED MOVING — MEASURED, NOT GUESSED. The parameters were never the
  reason, and two readings say so on their own. First: between two builds the chest stiffness
  went 2.60 -> 1.55, a 2.8x change in compliance, and the measured amplitude went 383.22 ->
  380.79. A spring whose amplitude does not answer to its own stiffness is not the thing
  governing that amplitude. Second: cycle 6 measured 558.10 on this very chain, and the number
  collapsed to ~380 in the same cycle that gave every family A volume a cap at the chain's own
  model pose. The device print names the mechanism: on keira-hd the per-chain minimum clearance
  reads cclr: 0=-0.0149 on every window, i.e. the capped torso volume sits PHYS-MODEL-CLR = 2
  units under the breast's modelled position, so the INWARD half of every jiggle cycle touches
  it. Contact sets lpush; lpush armed the family A model-pose bias (PHYS-MODELPULL, cycle 6 N);
  and that bias carried no motion gate whatsoever — only family==1 and lpush!=0. A swinging
  chest therefore lost a QUARTER of its deviation every frame: a 2.4-frame half-life, an
  artificial damping an order of magnitude stronger than any authored damping=, applied hardest
  exactly when the chain was moving most. It is also the mechanical definition of "FLASQUE".

  THE FIX IS A GATE, NOT A NUMBER. That bias is a REST mechanism: its job, and the reason it was
  written (owner cycle 6 — nothing may pass through its own character's mesh, with Maia's hair as
  the named case), is to stop a link the body is HOLDING from LIVING inside it, never to brake a
  link in transit. It now keeps its full 0.25 in exactly the two states it was built for — a link
  that has been fighting the same contact for PHYS-STICK frames, and a link that has gone quiet
  (ramping to full over PHYS-MPCALM = 4 frames of the same ccalm counter the arrival term reads) —
  and drops to PHYS-MPMOVE = 0.12 of its authority on a chain in free swing that merely grazes a
  volume. Maia's sustained-contact case keeps everything it had; a breast in mid-bounce keeps its
  bounce. The collision sweep still runs after this and still has the last word, so no penetration
  guarantee rests on the bias — which is precisely what makes the gate safe to add.

  FIRMNESS AND AMPLITUDE ARE NOW SEPARATE KNOBS (owner AB: "plus de jiggle ET un poil plus de
  fermete"; owner AG: "un poil trop JELLY... ronds et FERMES, elle est JEUNE"). Read together with
  the two verdicts before them, that request was arithmetically impossible under the old solver and
  that is worth saying plainly: a chain's ONLY excitation was the lag it develops behind its own
  moving target, and for a damped spring that lag is a/omega^2. Amplitude was therefore chained to
  1/stiffness^2. Every cycle that made the chest firmer made it move less ("ne bouge PAS ASSEZ"),
  and every cycle that made it move more made it floppier ("beaucoup plus FLASQUE"). The trade was
  in the model, not in the numbers chosen.
  So the model gained the term it was missing: `couple=`, the pseudo-force of the accelerating
  ANCHOR FRAME, measured as the anchor's own world position differenced twice and added as an
  explicit input. The chain now obeys d'' + 2*zeta*w*d' + w^2*d = -(1 + couple) * a_anchor, so
  amplitude is set by couple and firmness by stiffness, and the two stop fighting.
  Keira is consequently the FIRMEST chest in the file and the one that travels most per unit of
  body motion: stiffness=2.60 (omega_eff = 13.34 rad/s, 2.12 Hz — she snaps back, she does not
  wallow), damping=0.26 (zeta = 0.55, so it stops rather than rippling), stretch=0.05 (it does not
  deform much, which is what "ferme" means for a shape), and couple=2.2 for the travel. Maia keeps
  her heavier, slower spring (mass=4.2, omega_eff = 7.54 rad/s) AND gets the most coupling of the
  three (couple=2.8): she moves MORE than Keira and later than Keira, which is the owner's AD rule
  stated as physics rather than as a damping number. The bird lady sits between them at couple=1.6.
  couple= defaults to 0, so no chain outside these three feels this term at all.
