-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
17. TWO MEASUREMENTS THAT WERE NOT WHAT THEY LOOKED LIKE, AND THE DATA CHANGES THEY EXPLAIN
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) THE MODEL-POSE FIDELITY FIGURE CARRIES A STRUCTURAL TERM ON `anim=excite` CHAINS, and Daxter's
ears were the whole of the run's worst reading because of it. restdevA grades the position the BONE
receives against the model pose blended by `excite` toward the authored pose. The write-back,
however, hands the bone `target + influence * (sim - target)` — the per-link influence ramp is the
whole point of the gradient, and it is deliberately small at the root. At equilibrium the two
therefore differ by exactly
    excite * (1 - influence_root) * |authored - model|
for every family A chain in anim=excite mode, whatever the solver does. There are 40 such chains
(the beards, the moustaches, the tails, Daxter's ears, Maia's ponytail). Daxter's ears were the
worst instance measured, at 5.4026 and 5.5286 units, not because they fail to come home but because
his ears carry the LARGEST authored offset of the forty: excite=0.60 against an influence root of
0.55 leaves 27% of an offset that his animations drive to ~20 units.
The instrument is NOT being changed for this — the cycle-5 consigne is explicit that the counters
stay put, and the term is real: a root link genuinely does move less. What changed is the DATA, and
in the direction the owner already asked for. His cycle-3d verdict P on those exact chains was that
the physics stops too abruptly at mid-ear ("la transition est trop BRUTALE a mi-hauteur: ca fait un
CRAN bizarre") and his verdict D that too little of the length moves. dax-hd earL/earR go from
rootfree=0.5 to 0.90, so the root of the ear moves nearly as freely as the tip — a flatter influence
profile, a smaller step between neighbouring links, and, as a side effect, 78% less of the
structural term above.
(b) TOP PRECISION LEVEL: constraint iterations 8 -> 12. The owner's own specification for the
precision scale is that the highest level be the best realisable, listing "iterations de contrainte
plus nombreuses" among the levers, and Maia's back hair is the case that needs them: her strands
carry link radii saturated at the mesh-derived maximum (260 units) and she is the one actor whose
hair is in sustained contact with a torso for a whole cinematic. Levels 0 and 1 are untouched, so
this costs nothing on the low end of the scale.
