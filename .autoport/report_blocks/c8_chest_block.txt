17. (AK) WHY THE CHEST READ AS LIQUID — THE THREE LEVERS, SEPARATED AND MEASURED
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Owner, 2026-08-07 13:20, on the 12:41 build: "Les seins de Keira bougent BEAUCOUP PLUS
(excellent !) mais sont carrement LIQUIDES, c'est n'importe quoi, au moins 10 fois plus jelly
qu'avant."  Two statements, not one: the TRAVEL was right and the SHAPE was wrong.  Cycle 8 stops
treating them as one dial.

WHAT THE GEOMETRY ACTUALLY IS, MEASURED ON THE BAKED MODEL (not asserted):
  keira-hd    rBoob / lBoob   parent joint `chest`   link length  976.9 units
  evilsis     rboob / lbood   parent joint `chest`   link length 1083.2 units
  assistant   rBoob / lBoob   parent joint `chest`   link length 1112.8 units
Read out of decompiler_out/.../keira-highres-lod0.glb and evilsis-lod0.glb with the same loader
the collision derivation uses, so these are the numbers the runtime skins with.  A chest chain is
ONE link: it cannot bend, it can only pivot about that joint.

THE TWO THINGS `maxangle` WAS DOING AT ONCE.  The write-back (jak-hd-physics.gc, the swing block)
writes the full simulated TRANSLATION onto the bone and interpolates only its ROTATION by `swing=`.
So on a one-link chain:
    travel  = L * sqrt((1+stretch)^2 + 1 - 2*(1+stretch)*cos(maxangle))     <- what the eye reads
              as amplitude
    bone tip = angle( rest -> swing-interpolated direction )                <- what the eye reads
              as deformation, i.e. "liquid"
One key was setting both.  At maxangle=50 with swing=0.60 the bone was free to tip up to 30
degrees: a water balloon by construction, exactly as the supervisor's gate says, and no stiffness
value could have prevented it.

THE SETTING, AND WHY THIS ONE:
                       maxangle   bone tip   travel bound   owner's words
  keira-hd  chest        26 deg    15.6 deg     453.0 u     "ronds et FERMES", "ca bouge bien"
  evilsis   chest        26 deg    18.8 deg     531.9 u     "plus tombants, plus laches"
  bird-lady chest        26 deg       —         (unchanged, already inside the bound)
The owner asked for an excursion "de l'ordre de 15-22 degres".  15.6 degrees is the firm end of
his own band, and it is the BONE TIP — the quantity he was describing — not the solver's cone.
The travel bound of 453.0 units sits against the 459.7 units this same chain measured on the phone
on the build he called "bouge BEAUCOUP PLUS (excellent !)": the amplitude he approved is kept
within 1.5%, and the tip that made it liquid is halved.  Firmness is untouched — stiffness stays
2.60 and stretch 0.05, the 09:13 values he called the best version, and the gate refuses anything
softer.

AND THE AMPLITUDE IS BOUGHT WHERE THE OWNER SAID TO BUY IT.  "Obtenir la VIVACITE par le couplage
a l'ancre plus qu'en ouvrant la butee" — so `couple=` goes 2.2 -> 3.6 on Keira and 2.8 -> 4.2 on
Maia at the same time as the limit closes.  This is not a second dial doing the first one's job:
anchor coupling is a pseudo-force from the bust's own acceleration, bounded before it is applied
(no more than 0.35 of the chain's length as a second difference), so it changes how HARD the chest
is thrown, never how FAR it may go.  The point of raising it is that the travel then reaches its
limit on ordinary motion instead of depending on how vigorous the scene happens to be: the same
chain measured 383.2 units at maxangle=40 with no coupling and 459.7 at maxangle=50 with
couple=2.2, i.e. the drive, not the limit, was setting the amplitude, and a drive that varies with
the scene is why two builds could measure 80 units apart on the same data.  With the drive above
the limit the chest stops at 26 degrees and comes back, which is also the owner's own definition
of firm: "le mouvement s'arrete net et revient sur la forme du modele".

MAIA IS NOT A COPY OF KEIRA, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS ARGUED.  She is heavier (mass 4.2 against 1.5)
and her link is longer (1083.2 against 976.9), so at the same 26-degree limit she travels 531.9
units against Keira's 453.0 — she moves MORE, which is what the owner asked for after the damping
mistake, not less.  Her swing rises 0.45 -> 0.72 so her bone tips 18.8 degrees against Keira's
15.6: looser and softer in SHAPE, at the top of the owner's band, while her damping stays at 0.14,
BELOW Keira's 0.26.  Heavier means lower frequency and more lag, never more damping.

ONE HONEST NOTE ON SCOPE.  The gate is keyed on the chain NAME `chest*`, so it also caught
ogreboss-lod0's four chest ornaments (chestDanglerL/R, chestDangleL/R), which are family B hanging
pieces and not anatomy.  They went 38 -> 26 degrees with the rest.  26 degrees is still a natural
swing for a small hanging ornament, so this costs nothing visible, but it is a name-keyed change
rather than an anatomical one and it is recorded here as such rather than passed off as a finding.
