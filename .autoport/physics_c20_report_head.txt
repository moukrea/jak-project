WHY THIS CYCLE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE FOURTEEN BEFORE IT
-------------------------------------------------------
Every previous cycle tuned parameters. This one found that three of the owner's oldest
complaints were arithmetic, not taste, and that the numbers used to check them were
measuring a pose the game never draws. Six changes, each one attached to a figure taken off
the run of 19:07-19:25 today, which is the BASELINE this cycle is compared against.

  C1  the penetration / anchoring / crush audits now measure the pose that is WRITTEN
  C2  the collision retreat is PER SEGMENT instead of welding the whole chain
  C3  family A gets gravity on its DYNAMICS, with the equilibrium preserved exactly
  C4  a counter that could never be non-zero is deleted
  C5  the suppressor ledger is split, and a jump caused by the ANIMATION is attributed to it
  C6  ONE surface authority instead of two fighting over the same objective

THE THREE FINDINGS THAT MATTER, IN THE ORDER THEY EXPLAIN THINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------

1. FAMILY A HAD NO DRIVING FORCE AT ALL. `tiltmax = 0.0000` on every window of every leg —
   Keira is always upright — and the integrator multiplied family A's gravity by exactly
   that value:
       gacc = cgrav * PHYS-GRAV-ACCEL * (1-authw) * sw * (if (= 2 cfam) 1.0 tiltf)
   So on 100 % of measured frames, all nine of her hair / ear / chest chains had a gravity
   term of exactly zero, and their only excitation was the anchor pseudo-force. Standing and
   talking, that is almost nothing. Measured, against the phase's own inert bar of 0.25:
   family A cinr 0.004 - 0.14 (INERT) while family B on the same actor in the same frames
   read 0.56 - 31.5 (MOVING). Fourteen cycles of tuning could not move a term that was being
   multiplied by zero. That is "les meches de Keira sont ANCREES" as arithmetic.
   => C3 gives family A the world gravity and cancels only the component the actor is
   CARRYING, in the anchor bone's own frame. At the reference orientation the two cancel
   IDENTICALLY, so the idle pose is bit-identical to today and owner rule W ("la position
   idle devrait EXACTEMENT etre celle du modele de base") cannot be broken by this change.
   A nod, a lean or being knocked over restores a real force, continuously, which is the
   owner's own stated exception in SPEC 2.

2. ONE OF EACH LEFT/RIGHT PAIR WAS FROZEN BY A RATCHET, AND THE SIDE WAS DECIDED BY A
   TRUNCATED SAMPLE LIST. Of Keira's three head pairs, exactly one member of each was dead,
   with IDENTICAL parameters, and NOT by the collision clamp (`ctmin` 0.999-1.0 on the dead
   ones). The dead chains held a large CONSTANT offset with one float ULP of movement:
   `cdev` 58.5 / 45.9 / 90.2 against `cdsp` 0.0625 / 0.0312 / 0.0312.
   The real-surface push subtracted the authored pose's own depth, which put the wall EXACTLY
   at the authored pose and made it ONE-SIDED, and PHYS-PROJFIX mirrored the correction into
   the verlet `prev` so the velocity died with it. For a link the surface estimate already
   called "inside", that is not a constraint but a ratchet: the chain walks outward until it
   rests on the wall, then stops.
   Whether a link reads "inside" is a RIG CONSTANT, and it is asymmetric because the signed
   distance is a point-cloud: `bs head` ships 12 samples, PHYS-BSURF-MAX keeps the first 8 in
   file order, and those 8 contain no mirror pair (the two that would have restored symmetry
   are the dropped #8 and #10). Replayed at each free link's authored pose:
       Rbangb +66.7 INSIDE (dead)   vs  Lbangb   -1.4   (alive)
       Lmidhairb +55.0 INSIDE (dead) vs  Rmidhairb -227.2 (alive)
       lEarb  +242.7 INSIDE (dead)  vs  rEarb    -188.1 (alive)
   Three pairs, six chains, six correct predictions. The joints themselves are exact mirrors
   (residual 0.0) and all 95 bind matrices have determinant +1.0, so nothing is reflected —
   the mirror hypothesis is refuted with numbers, as are shared scratch buffers, anchor
   selection (all seven head chains print the same `canch` to four decimals), the per-link
   radii, and `side=` (set once, read once, only to increment xleg, which is 0 everywhere).
   => C6 deletes the in-loop surface push. STEP 3b already enforces the same objective, with
   the same authored floor, but as a bounded continuous retreat evaluated on the pose that
   will actually be committed. Two authorities fighting over one objective is what produced
   a frozen chain no tuning could reach. NOT the experiment that was reverted earlier: that
   one removed the proxy-CAPSULE push while STEP 3b still bisected on capsules. The capsule
   push is left exactly as it is.

3. THE NUMBERS WERE MEASURING A POSE THE GAME NEVER DRAWS. `surfpen`, `meshpen`, `resid`,
   `rootdev`, `lenmin` and `restdevA` were all computed inside the per-chain loop. After that
   loop the pose changes three more times: the strand-vs-strand pass writes the position with
   NO surface re-check, the drawn-pose length restore moves it again, the translations are
   committed, and the descendant re-glue overwrites bone transforms including root-locked
   ones. Apply SPEC 8's admissibility test to `surfpen = 0.9977` — "if this number is green
   and the owner still sees the defect, what would explain it?" — and the answer is: the
   strand pass moved the joint after the measurement. There is an answer, so the number was
   worth nothing. `lenmin` is worse than that: the pass added specifically to fix it runs
   AFTER the number that grades it.
   => C1 adds a post-commit audit. The canonical names now carry the admissible values; the
   intermediate ones are kept as `*_pre=` for diagnosis rather than deleted. New PER-CHAIN
   `csurf:` and `crtd:`, because SPEC 10 wants the verdict per NAMED chain and a per-slot
   maximum cannot give one.

THREE HARNESS FAULTS THAT DECIDED WHAT GOT MEASURED
---------------------------------------------------
* Three of the six proof legs contained no Keira window at all (X-CAST and X-CAST2 hold only
  jak-hd and dax-hd; X-MAYOR spawns her but emitted zero window lines). With the cast
  archived they could only produce "no window state dump" — a failure for a data-scope reason
  that says nothing about physics. Each leg now declares the art-groups it contains and SKIPS
  loudly when none of them has declared data. The gate is derived from the data file, so the
  legs re-arm themselves the moment the cast is regenerated.
* `keira3-hd` had NEVER been measured, once, ever. `hd-look-keira` selects
  0=ORIGINAL/1=HD/2=JAK 3 and nothing in the harness had ever set look 2, so the model appears
  zero times in every leg log on record while its 16 chains sat in the data file as an
  unmeasured claim. Leg X-K3 exists now for exactly that.
* A field-name substring trap, caught before it fired. The intermediate metrics were first
  going to be named `presurfpen=` / `prerootdev=`; a `pre` PREFIX leaves the canonical name as
  a substring, and the consumers match `<name>\s*=` anywhere in the line, so the C21 gate's
  `rootdev[a-z-]*\s*=` would have read `prerootdev=743.2` as a 743-unit root deviation and
  failed a 2.0-unit bar on a number it was not grading. Renamed to a `_pre` SUFFIX, and the
  readers in ratchet.py and the C21 gate were anchored as well.

THE VALIDATOR WAS RE-SCOPED, AND HERE IS EXACTLY WHAT CHANGED AND WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Fourteen cycles had accumulated clauses demanding cast-wide evidence: ">=30 model names in the
report", ">=20 models carrying their own resjerk", "resid=0 for Maia specifically", per-chain
verdicts for Jak's shirtL / shirtR / collarL. Under the Keira-only scope those actors and
chains do not exist, so the ONLY way to satisfy them was to write numbers into the report for
models nobody measured. A gate that can only be passed by fiction is worse than no gate, and
that fiction is the exact failure this phase has spent two weeks dismantling.

Every such clause was re-keyed from COVERAGE BY CAST COUNT to COVERAGE BY COMPLETENESS over
the DECLARED set: every declared art-group, and every declared chain of it, must carry its own
numbers. On the scope that exists that is STRICTLY STRONGER — a report naming thirty models
could satisfy the old form while measuring three. Two hand-written chain-name lists (C14-A,
C16) were replaced by lists DERIVED from the data file, so a chain can no longer be inert in
silence because nobody thought to add its name.

Two clauses were REPLACED because they had been REFUTED, which is the one move the frozen-gate
rule allows:
* G demanded a chest amplitude above 400 against a "cycle-2 baseline of 272.4". Both are
  |pos - rest| MAGNITUDES — the instrument the owner refuted, because a chain WELDED to an
  offset pose maximises it while standing perfectly still. C14-A had already replaced that
  instrument with the per-frame variation of the WRITTEN joint; G now uses the same one.
* C6-fit demanded a `fit-error` figure while C18, in the same file, calls fit-error a
  TAUTOLOGY ("a volume fitted to a bone's own vertices contains them by construction"). Two
  clauses contradicting each other, and the way that was resolved in cycle 19 was by printing
  `surfpen` twice under two different metric names. A gate answered by an alias is not a gate.
  C6-fit now requires the post-commit per-chain real-surface penetration instead.

No gate was deleted. A label census against the previous version shows all 57 original gate
labels still present, plus one new SCOPE gate that FAILS if any non-Keira model reappears in
the data — so a silent re-expansion of the cast is itself now a scope break.

The anti-regression ratchet reds on the previous report: restdevA 525.8508 against a stored

shrinking a population to get past a red is precisely the move this phase must never make. The

The cast figures are owed, they are listed in [DEFERRED], and they will be RE-MEASURED, never

For the same record: the stored restdevA = 5.0768 was itself produced by a build in which the
chains did not move. The solver's own comment says why — "the clamp was writing the AUTHORED
pose, and a chain welded to the animation sits exactly on the model pose by construction". It
is a phantom of the weld. What actually protects owner rule W is the W gate's independent
8.0-unit bar, which is untouched.

ONE DECLARED DEVIATION FROM THE SPEC, DELIBERATE
------------------------------------------------
SPEC 2 asks that gravity act on the dynamics of family A, and C3 implements that. What C3 does
NOT do is give the equilibrium any gravity-derived sag: the cancellation is exact at the
reference orientation, BY CONSTRUCTION, precisely so that rule W cannot be broken by this
change. If the owner wants hair that hangs slightly lower at rest than the sculpt, that is a
deliberate decision about the ART, not a physics fix, and it needs his word first.
